# Self-Image

**Summary**: The mental blueprint of "the kind of person I am" — built from authoritative-source × intensity × repetition, treated by the [servo-mechanism](./automatic-success-mechanism.md) as the *target* that filters every work-order it receives. The self-image is the **upstream gate** of every reflex the wiki's [automaticity pipeline](./automaticity-and-reflex-training.md) tries to build. No drill pushes the learner past the [snap-back line](./snap-back-effect.md) the self-image holds. This page is the wiki's owner page for the concept; canonical source is [psycho-cybernetics-maltz](./psycho-cybernetics-maltz.md) Ch 1, 4, 7, 10, 11, 15.

## Definition

The self-image is the mental picture each person holds of "the sort of person I am" — built unconsciously from past experiences, successes and failures, others' reactions (especially in early childhood), and the interpretations layered on top of those facts. (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 1)

> "Whether we realize it or not, each of us carries within us a mental blueprint or picture of ourselves. It may be vague and ill-defined to our conscious gaze. In fact, it may not be consciously recognizable at all. But it is there, complete down to the last detail." (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 1)

Once a belief about oneself enters this picture, it becomes "truth" for the bearer — not questioned, just acted upon. All actions, feelings, behaviour, and even abilities remain consistent with this self-image. Maltz: *always*.

## Why the self-image is upstream of automaticity

The wiki's [automaticity pipeline](./automaticity-and-reflex-training.md) runs through the Great Work's 7 operations and ends at Coagulation (Automate). Every operation assumes the learner is a fixed substrate on which the operation works. Maltz refutes this: **the learner is itself a substrate**, namely the self-image, that pre-filters what each operation can deliver.

| Pipeline operation | Self-image filter |
|---|---|
| Calcination (Perceive) | "I am the kind of person who notices this signal" → if no, signal bypasses |
| Dissolution (Classify) | "I am competent to name this type" → if no, mis-routes |
| Separation (Represent) | "I have the mental equipment to model this" → if no, model collapses |
| Conjunction (Select) | "I'm the kind of person who picks this rule" → if no, picks a safer rule |
| Fermentation (Execute) | "I can do this move" → if no, the move stays conceptual |
| Distillation (Feedback) | "I can hear correction without ego-injury" → if no, feedback is rejected |
| **Coagulation (Automate)** | **"This response belongs to me" → if no, [snap-back](./snap-back-effect.md)** |

The snap-back fires at Coagulation. The fix is not more drilling — the drill has already produced the response. The fix is at the self-image layer, upstream of the drill.

This makes the self-image the wiki's missing **step 0** above Calcination. See [psycho-cybernetics-maltz](./psycho-cybernetics-maltz.md) §"Connection to the highest phase of learning".

## How the self-image is built — the three levers

Maltz names the three encoders that imprint material into the self-image (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 1, "limbic memory or self-image imprinting"):

| Lever | What it is | Wiki connection |
|---|---|---|
| **Authoritative source** | A figure the subject accepts as credible (parent, coach, teacher, expert, internal "self-talk" once it claims the source-role) | matches modern source-credibility research; [HEART](./heart-overview.md) Recognition slot detects when a person is functioning as authoritative source |
| **Intensity** | Emotional charge at the moment of imprinting (yelling, public humiliation, peak success) | matches LeDoux on amygdala-mediated consolidation priority; [remaps](./remaps.md) Sensations move provides intensity for constructive identity-rehearsal |
| **Repetition** | Rate of return to the same affirmation / experience | matches [spaced-repetition](./spaced-repetition.md); [theater-of-the-mind](./theater-of-the-mind.md) is *deliberate* repetition under self-as-authoritative-source |

The combination is multiplicative, not additive. A single high-authority + high-intensity event with no repetition can imprint as deeply as years of low-intensity repetition. This is why a single humiliation by a parent at age 8 can govern adult behaviour 30 years later — and why the wiki recommends [Theater of the Mind](./theater-of-the-mind.md) as the deliberate counter-protocol: turn yourself into the authoritative source, dial up intensity through [remaps](./remaps.md) Sensations, and repeat daily.

## The audit — Self-Image Inventory

The first operation on the self-image is **inventory**: surface the contents so they're available for editing. The audit is structured around the phrase "I am the kind of person who…" — completed many times until the repeating patterns surface. (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 14, "I am the kind of person who…" template)

Audit protocol:

1. **Topic seed** — pick the domain where progress has plateaued (study, sales, relationships, fitness, public speaking, money). Note: the plateau is the indicator that the self-image is the binding constraint.
2. **Sentence completion** — write 20 sentences starting "I am the kind of person who…" without editing.
3. **Polarity scan** — mark each sentence as `+` (serves the goal), `−` (opposes the goal), or `?` (ambiguous).
4. **Origin trace** — for each `−`, identify the authoritative-source × intensity × repetition combination that imprinted it. Often a single childhood incident emerges. (Maltz: "you were told by your father at age ten…")
5. **Cancel** — when the same sentence resurfaces in daily self-talk, yell `Cancel!` internally and substitute the constructive affirmation. Repetition under self-as-authoritative-source overrides the old imprint over weeks. (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 4)
6. **Theater rehearsal** — feed the constructive affirmation into [theater-of-the-mind](./theater-of-the-mind.md) for 21 consecutive days.

**Pass-floor**: ≥6 of 8 weeks with audit + cancel events logged. **METER metric**: weekly-review hit-rate.

## Failure modes

1. **Inventory paralysis** — running audit forever without rehearsing the constructive affirmations. The audit is a *means*, not an end. Pass-floor: ≤2 weeks in audit before starting first Theater cycle.
2. **Affirmation without rehearsal** — writing constructive sentences and never living the imagined scene. The self-image updates on rehearsed *experience*, not on stated *propositions*.
3. **Rehearsing with wrong scene-register** — running identity scenes on Velvet Aeon's *romantic-ruin* preserve primes the failure mechanism. Use [world-velvet-aeon](./world-velvet-aeon.md) §Identity sub-mode.
4. **Skipping the origin trace** — without tracing the authoritative-source × intensity × repetition combination that imprinted the negative belief, the constructive affirmation can't break through. The trace exposes the imprint's mechanism, which weakens it.
5. **Importing parental authority into adulthood** — many self-image rules from age 8 are still tagged with "Dad said so" credibility. Adult-self must be promoted to authoritative source.
6. **Drilling forever past the snap-back line** — the load-bearing failure mode this whole page exists to prevent. See [snap-back-effect](./snap-back-effect.md).

## Mnemonic and checksum

**Mnemonic**: a brass-framed mirror in a Velvet-Aeon temple, scratched with old graffiti from authoritative voices — you stand before it with a soft cloth (rehearsal) and polish one phrase at a time, watching the surface clear and the figure in the mirror settle into the kind of person you're becoming.

**Checksum**: complete "I am the kind of person who…" with three sentences (one for the current dominant identity, one for the limiting belief surfaced in audit, one for the constructive substitute being rehearsed in [theater-of-the-mind](./theater-of-the-mind.md)). If all three answer in under 30 seconds, the self-image audit is live.

## Self-image as attack surface (2026-05-27, Lesli ingest)

The same Maltz mechanism that lets *you* deliberately install a self-image in yourself is the mechanism a hostile IDSE operator uses to install a fabricated self-image in *you*. The wiki names this attack as frame-projection-attack (operator's own doctrine, from Lesli's *Волшебная таблетка / Magic Pill*: "the first law of magic: you create a non-existent image in her consciousness; what matters is not what is; what matters is what you have drawn in her head").

The mechanisms compose:

| Maltz lever (defensive use) | Lesli weaponisation (operator-side) | Defense |
|---|---|---|
| Authoritative source | Operator positions self as the "I know who you really are" authority | Refuse the authority claim; treat operator's identity-reads as data about *them*, not about you |
| Intensity | Operator drives target emotion via 4-axis rotation; high-intensity moments imprint the operator-installed identity | SHIELD per-statement; refuse to verbalise the operator's framing |
| Repetition | Operator reinforces installed frame across multiple interactions; closed community (style-of-life-isolation) supplies social-proof repetition | Outside-witness check (RAP §Layer 3 pre-explain test); keep external reference frames active |

**The Cancel! protocol generalises**: the same internal `Cancel!` pattern-interrupt that intercepts negative self-talk from *internal* sources also works against operator-installed identity claims from *external* sources. When a counterparty asserts "you are the kind of person who…" — Cancel! it, internally, with the same vigour as you would an ANT.

The wiki treatment: this page (self-image) is the upstream identity-shaping layer that the operator targets; frame-projection-attack is the attack catalog; [failure-mechanism](./failure-mechanism.md) §Cancel! is the per-statement defense; RAP is the relationship-tier defense. They compose into a bidirectional identity-protection stack.
