## Boundary pair design — the N/S dyad

Cemetery (SW, contemplative angels) and Mardaleishvili (NW, frenetic doctor) form a **tonally inverted pair** along the western edge of the district:

| Pair element | Cemetery (S) | Mardaleishvili (N) |
|---|---|---|
| Activity | static, silent rotation | frenetic, kinetic catapulting |
| Sound | low hum | air-whoosh + unfurling fabric |
| Light | faint blue eye-glow | bright daylight + white streamers |
| Tone | contemplative | urgent |
| Direction-arrow | inward (heads turn toward metro/centre) | outward then back (streamers go SE toward home-locus) |

The inversion is deliberate: confusable boundary hubs (both "western edge cemetery-or-clinic medical-adjacent things") get **maximally distinct REMAPS sensory channels** so they never collapse. This is the NEDF Distinguisher discipline applied to boundary-pair design.
