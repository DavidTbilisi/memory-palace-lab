# Surf vs Dive Learning Domains

**Summary**: The two **depth-of-reflection** layers a strategic thinker operates in. The **surf domain** handles instrumental, transactional, "what / how" learning — fast, mechanical, predictable, debate-and-discussion friendly. The **dive domain** handles communicative, deep-dive, "why / whose frame" learning — slow, iterative, intuitive, requires critical inquiry + dialogue + reflection. A third **transformative domain** can fire from either, triggered by a disorienting dilemma. The axis is **orthogonal** to the [Triad dots](./sloan-triad-model.md) and to [Lamp/Scale/Sword](./automaticity-and-reflex-training.md) — the wiki gains a new operating axis.

**Sources**:

## The three domains side-by-side

| Domain | Description of learning | Common meaning-making techniques | Significance to strategic thinking |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Surf** | Linear · sequential · progressive · mechanistic · predictable · transactional · instrumental · routine · instructional · informational · *"avoid surprises"* · "what" + "how-to" | Analysis · tactical reasoning · logical reasoning · identify correct/incorrect · exchange data · reporting · feasibility studies · problem-solving · decision-making · **debate** · **discussion** | Convergence-oriented · positions formulated and defended · opponents/proponents identified · competitive data collection · analysis · closure |
| **Dive** | Nonlinear · intuitive · rational and/or a-rational · strong affective component · *"invite surprises"* · "my opinion" + "my beliefs" + "my values" + "my conviction" + "my principle" + "why" + "what else" | Critical reflection · dialectic thinking · metaphoric speech · analogic thinking · storytelling · collaborative inquiry · critical questioning · imagining · improvising · experimenting · expanding perspectives · testing · challenging · creating · **critical dialogue** | Divergence-oriented · messy · continuously changing · open to new ideas + perspectives · imaginative · surprising |
| **Transformative** (can fire from surf or dive) | Triggered by **disorientation** or **catastrophe** · "why" · "how" · "what next" · "what else" · "why not" · "what if" | Critical reflection · critical dialogue | Transformative · dramatic shift · unpredictable · **shatter frames** · reveal assumptions · test premise · expand assumptions |

## Why this is a new axis

The wiki already has two operating axes:

1. **[Triad dot](./sloan-triad-model.md)** — which strategy mode is in focus (Thinking · Planning · Implementation)
2. **[Lamp/Scale/Sword](./automaticity-and-reflex-training.md)** — pressure of execution (Recognition · Discrimination · Pressure)

Surf vs dive adds a third independent axis: **depth of reflection**. Together the three axes give a 3D operating space, not a one-dimensional ladder.

| Example combination | What it means |
|---|---|
| Thinking · Dive · Lamp | New problem; you are exploring frames at depth, under low time pressure |
| Planning · Surf · Sword | Executing the plan under cold pressure; instrumental + fast |
| Implementation · Surf · Sword | Doing the thing; routine response under pressure |
| Thinking · Transformative · Sword | The strategy collapsed mid-execution; a disorienting dilemma forces frame-shatter |
| Planning · Dive · Lamp | You are planning, but pausing to ask "is this plan addressing the real problem?" |

The Thinking dot **does not require** dive (you can surf-level think superficially); the Planning dot **does not preclude** dive (a planner can pause to question premise). The axis is genuinely independent.

## Surf is undervalued by Sloan — corrected here

Sloan emphasizes the dive domain so heavily that surf reads as second-class. The wiki correction: **surf is not inferior; it is the only correct domain for many tasks**. Specifically:

- Operational tasks ([Earth-element](./automaticity-and-reflex-training.md) skills) belong in surf
- Routine drill execution belongs in surf
- Compliance, audit, regulatory work belongs in surf
- Most of the daily loop runs in surf — the dive moves are *interruptions*, not the baseline

The dive domain's value is **proportional to the stakes and the novelty of the problem**. For high-stakes novel problems, dive is non-substitutable. For routine problems, dive is wasted overhead — and over-diving can paralyze action ("analysis paralysis" is over-diving on a problem that needs surf-level execution).

## The transformative domain — when it fires

Triggered by:
- **Disorienting dilemma** (Mezirow's term) — an experience that contradicts the existing frame and cannot be accommodated within it
- **Catastrophe** — sudden disaster forcing a re-evaluation of foundational assumptions
- **Major gain** — a surprising success that re-frames what's possible (Sloan's example: realizing your income has ballooned past your parents')

The transformative experience is what enables [memory-reconsolidation](./memory-reconsolidation.md)-level frame change. It is rare, often painful, and is the *opportunity* for innovative strategy. Without it, strategists tend to extend existing frames rather than create new ones.

**Critical caveat** (Sloan): *experiencing* a catastrophe does not guarantee transformative learning. The learner must engage critical reflection + critical dialogue during and after the event. Many catastrophes produce only trauma, not learning.

## How to know which domain you're in

| Signal | Domain |
|---|---|
| You're asking "what / how" questions | Surf |
| You're asking "why / whose frame / what else / why not" questions | Dive |
| You can articulate the answer in a sentence with data | Surf |
| You can't articulate the answer, but you have a strong feeling about the frame | Dive |
| You just had an experience that contradicts what you believed yesterday | Transformative |
| You feel comfortable; ideas flow easily; the meeting is on rails | Surf |
| You feel agitated, uncertain, slightly resistant; the conversation diverges | Dive |
| You feel disoriented, shaken, unable to retreat to old frames | Transformative |

## METER

- `surf_dive_ratio` — fraction of session-level questions in dive vs surf; pass ≥30% dive for strategic-thinking sessions, ≤10% dive for execution sessions
- `transformative_event_log` — record disorienting dilemmas; review monthly for under-extraction of learning
- `over_dive_flag` — diving on a routine surf-domain task; pass <1 event/week

## Mnemonic

A **two-tier seascape** in Velvet Aeon Environment-mode. The **surface** is a long horizontal sweep — sun-bright, surfers gliding along an unbroken wave, all moving in the same direction (instrumental, transactional, debate-friendly). One surfer wears a clipboard and a digital watch; another carries a ruler. Below, the water turns deeper blue, then black. A single diver descends slowly, **head down**, **a coil of question marks trailing from her hand** (the 9 dive questions). Around her, **strange creatures** (the dive domain's a-rational substrate) float in non-sequenced clusters. **Far below** the diver, a third figure curls in **luminous embryonic posture** at the seabed — the Transformative state, fragile and just-born, triggered by the *fissure in the seabed* (the disorienting dilemma) that opened beside her. The surface, dive, and seabed are visible in one frame, separated by two hard horizons (the cluster boundaries). The lighting: sun above, a single bioluminescent organ at depth, no particulate noise. The diver has long hair lifting in the current; her face is fragile/luminous.
