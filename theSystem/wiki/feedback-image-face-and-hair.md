# Face & Hair Feedback — the STRONG / FRAGILE Figure Doctrine

**Summary**: The render rule that governs the *face* and *hair* of every human figure in a Neural OS mnemonic image. It is a user-feedback doctrine (the `feedback_*` image-rule family) with two load-bearing decisions: (1) the figure's face is one of two archetypes — **STRONG** or **FRAGILE/luminous** — and the choice is not decorative, it *encodes a property of the concept being remembered* (is mastering this skill a power move or a care move?); (2) the hair is always long and lifted, and a **hair-and-water effect** marks the sorrow/dissolution sub-register. This page is the owner of the doctrine that ~18 wiki image-prompts already cite as `feedback_image_face_and_hair`.

## The core binary — STRONG vs FRAGILE

Every figure's face is rendered as one of two archetypes. The render specs are fixed; the *selection* carries the meaning.

| | **STRONG archetype** | **FRAGILE / luminous archetype** |
|---|---|---|
| **Face** | Angular jaw, piercing gaze, milky-white skin; jaw *set* | Luminous-porcelain, soft; often head tilted upward; on the edge of a tear |
| **Reads as** | Power, command, must-win, force | Care, precision, patience, restraint, mourning |
| **Encodes a concept whose mastery is a…** | **power move** — you exert force against the problem | **care move** — you withhold force; the skill is *not* reaching |
| **Canonical dress / light** | Standing at a board/bench, working garment, single warm lamp | White shift/gown, single overhead light, empty sky |

The discipline: **the face is not chosen by the figure's gender or beauty — it is chosen by asking "is getting good at this thing a matter of power or a matter of care?"** That question has a definite answer for most skills, and the face is its visual cache.

## The selection rule is a Distinguisher

This is the doctrine's real point, and why it earns a page rather than a style note. The STRONG/FRAGILE choice is a [NEDF](./nedf-overview.md) **Distinguisher** applied to a human face: it forces a discriminating feature onto the figure that is *false for the opposite kind of skill*. A learner who later recalls "the scribe was fragile, almost weeping" has cached "proof construction is precise and careful, not forceful" — the face did encoding work, not just decoration. A STRONG face on a precision skill would be a Distinguisher error: it would cache the wrong property.

Worked contrast from the wiki's own pages:
- [universal-mathematical-tactics](./universal-mathematical-tactics.md) → **STRONG** ("the four tactics are *power moves*").
- [methods-of-mathematical-argument](./methods-of-mathematical-argument.md) → **FRAGILE** ("proof construction is precise, careful, luminous-porcelain rather than power").
- [red-herring-resistance](./red-herring-resistance.md) → **FRAGILE** ("resistance to seduction is a fragile, not a power, skill — it requires the patience to *not* reach").
- [crux-move](./crux-move.md) → **STRONG** ("the crux requires power not fragility").

## Face / stance decoupling — the hybrid case

Face and *stance* are two independent axes. Face encodes the **nature of the skill**; stance encodes the **posture the moment demands**. They usually agree, but for **tension/paradox concepts they deliberately disagree** — a fragile face on a strong stance:

- [and-polarity-thinking](./and-polarity-thinking.md) → "fragile/luminous (per face/hair) **but the stance is strong**" — the figure holds an unresolvable polarity on a narrow bridge; the *holding* is forceful, the *spirit* is care.
- [sloan-learning-to-think-strategically](./sloan-learning-to-think-strategically.md) → "fragile in the face but strong in the stance" — strategy as imagination (fragile) executed with must-win commitment (strong).

The hybrid is the doctrine's signature for **AND-polarity / dialectical-tension** subjects: the visual itself refuses to collapse to one pole.

## The hair rule

Hair is the constant that makes a figure recognizable as a Neural OS mnemonic figure regardless of archetype:

- **Always long** — waist-length or trailing to the desk/floor.
- **Lifted in wind** (cosmic wind, canyon wind, dome wind) for Cosmic-register scenes; **flowing down** for Mortal/Environment-register scenes.
- Shining under the single warm light source (which is itself the [Velvet Aeon](./world-velvet-aeon.md) light discipline).

## The hair-and-water effect — the sorrow sub-register

A specific, sparing effect: **hair-tips trailing into shallow water**, or **a single tear fallen onto wet stone**. It marks the Velvet Aeon **sorrow-as-guidance** preserve — loss, dissolution, the mourning of an over-reach — and must be used *only* where that note is intended, never as default:

- [red-herring-resistance](./red-herring-resistance.md) → "hair tips trail into shallow water … dissolving the temptation of decoration."
- [methods-of-mathematical-argument](./methods-of-mathematical-argument.md) → "one tear has fallen onto the wet stone of Tablet 3."
- [world-velvet-aeon](./world-velvet-aeon.md) §Identity → the water/dissolution effect "applies sparingly — used to mark *transitions* between identity states, not the default."

## Anti-patterns

- **Decorative archetype choice** — picking STRONG because the figure "looks better" rather than because the skill is a power move. The face stops encoding and becomes noise.
- **Water effect as default** — using hair-in-water everywhere drains its meaning; it must stay scarce so it reliably reads as *sorrow/transition*.
- **Forcing agreement on a tension concept** — collapsing a polarity subject to a single archetype erases the dialectic the hybrid is there to preserve.

## METER hooks

- `image.archetype_justified` — for any new figure prompt, was the STRONG/FRAGILE choice tied to a power-vs-care reading of the concept (not aesthetics)?
- `image.water_effect_scarcity` — fraction of figure-prompts using the hair-and-water effect; should stay low (sorrow is the exception, not the register).
