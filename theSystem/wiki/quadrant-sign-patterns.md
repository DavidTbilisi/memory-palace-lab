# Quadrant Sign Patterns

**Summary**: A concept page for retrieving sine and cosine signs by quadrant using directional polarity on the unit-circle compass.

**Sources**:
- raw/01 Core_Memory/Math/Trigonometry.md
- eye-movement-and-compass-mnemonics.md

**Last updated**: 2026-04-30

---

## Core Rule

The source connects quadrant sign behavior directly to compass polarity. East/west controls cosine because cosine is the horizontal coordinate, and north/south controls sine because sine is the vertical coordinate. (source: raw/01 Core_Memory/Math/Trigonometry.md)

Using the coordinate rule:

`(x, y) = (cos theta, sin theta)`

the sign pattern follows immediately from position on the circle. (source: raw/01 Core_Memory/Math/Trigonometry.md)

## Sign Table

| Quadrant | Direction | sin | cos | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | NE | + | + | east is positive x, north is positive y |
| Q2 | NW | + | - | west is negative x, north is positive y |
| Q3 | SW | - | - | west is negative x, south is negative y |
| Q4 | SE | - | + | east is positive x, south is negative y |

This reframes the sign table as a direct consequence of location rather than a separate memorization burden. (source: raw/01 Core_Memory/Math/Trigonometry.md)

## Emotional Encoding Layer

The note also uses emotionally distinct diagonal images to give each quadrant a different feel:

- NE: straw, clean and fully positive
- NW: sick nose, mixed with something wrong
- SW: man in soup, heavy and fully negative
- SE: cookie in milk, mixed but calmer

Those images are not the math itself. They are retrieval cues for the sign pattern attached to each quadrant. (source: raw/01 Core_Memory/Math/Trigonometry.md)

## Practical Use

If you forget a sign, do not search memory for a raw table. First place the angle into its quadrant, then recover the sign from horizontal and vertical polarity. (source: raw/01 Core_Memory/Math/Trigonometry.md)

## Gaze Rule

You can reinforce the sign logic with eye movement:

- rightward gaze reinforces positive cosine
- leftward gaze reinforces negative cosine
- upward gaze reinforces positive sine
- downward gaze reinforces negative sine

Diagonal gaze then acts as the quadrant selector. This makes the sign pattern feel like orientation rather than recitation. (source: eye-movement-and-compass-mnemonics.md)
