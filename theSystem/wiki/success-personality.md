# Success Personality — the S·U·C·C·E·S·S. Mnemonic

**Summary**: Maltz's 7-trait composite of the "Success-type" personality — the configuration that triggers the [Automatic Success Mechanism](./automatic-success-mechanism.md) to run in its forward-mode. The mnemonic is built into the word itself: **S**ense of direction · **U**nderstanding · **C**ourage · **C**harity · **E**steem · **S**elf-confidence · **S**elf-acceptance. The 7 traits are not personality "givens" — they are *acquirable*, by deliberate practice and self-image rewiring. Canonical source: [psycho-cybernetics-maltz](./psycho-cybernetics-maltz.md) Ch 8.

## The 7 traits

(source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 8, "Ingredients of the 'Success-Type' Personality")

| Letter | Trait | One-line essence |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | **S**ense of direction | A clear, present-tense target the [ASM](./automatic-success-mechanism.md) can steer toward |
| **U** | **U**nderstanding | Accurate read of one's own situation and others' communications; refuses to act on misread data |
| **C** | **C**ourage | Willingness to act on incomplete information and to bear the cost of mistakes |
| **C** | **C**harity (compassion) | Treating others as having worth; the operational dual of self-esteem |
| **E** | **E**steem | Holding oneself (and others) as worthy of dignity and capable of growth |
| **S** | **S**elf-confidence | Felt assurance built on stored successes; trained not declared |
| **S** | **S**elf-acceptance | Acknowledging one's actual mix of strengths / weaknesses / accomplishments / unrealized potential, without rejection |

The word *spells itself* as the mnemonic. Three **S**'s frame the structure: Sense of direction (the target), Self-confidence (the felt readiness), Self-acceptance (the substrate that allows the other two to be real).

---

## Why this triggers the ASM

The [ASM](./automatic-success-mechanism.md) runs forward (success-mode) when the [self-image](./self-image.md) supplies it with a clear target and authorises the action. Each S·U·C·C·E·S·S. trait removes one specific blocker:

| Trait | Blocker it removes |
|---|---|
| Sense of direction | "No target" — ASM has nothing to steer toward |
| Understanding | "Wrong target" — acting on misread data |
| Courage | "Frozen at the start" — fear of error clamps the corrective loop |
| Charity | "Hostile environment" — treating others as worthless poisons the field the ASM operates in |
| Esteem | "I am not worthy of success" — self-image rejects the work-order |
| Self-confidence | "I cannot do this" — ASM authorisation withheld |
| Self-acceptance | "I am broken and must be fixed first" — rejection of the substrate the ASM runs on |

The negative dual is the [F·A·I·L·U·R·E.](./failure-mechanism.md) signal pattern. The two mnemonics are paired diagnostics for the same servomechanism in opposite modes.

## Per-trait operational definitions

### Sense of direction

> "Functioning humans must have goals to ride on, like a torpedo or a missile is set to its target." (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 8)

Operationally: a single named target in present-tense, "already exists" framing (as in [ASM](./automatic-success-mechanism.md) §AIM). Without it, the ASM idles. Pass-floor: name the target without hesitation when asked.

**Wiki connection**: [problem-solving-os](./problem-solving-os.md) step 2 (Classify and frame); [ARC](./arc-framework.md) Assess phase.

### Understanding

> "Many doctors believe that 'confusion' is the basic element in neurosis. To deal effectively with a problem, you must have some understanding of its true nature. Most of our failures in human relations are due to misunderstandings." (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 8)

Operationally: ask "what is *actually* happening here?" before "what should I do?" Refuses to act on second-hand interpretations.

**Wiki connection**: [5-gates-of-comprehension](./5-gates-of-comprehension.md); [bridge-load](./bridge-load.md) step 1 (gather the target situation accurately before mapping).

### Courage

> "We often think of courage in terms of heroic deeds on the battlefield… But everyday living requires courage too." (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 8)

Operationally: willingness to act on incomplete information, accepting that errors *are* the medium through which the corrective loop runs ([ASM](./automatic-success-mechanism.md) §RELAX). Trained by acting bravely in small things; transfers to larger ones.

**Wiki connection**: [automatic-success-mechanism](./automatic-success-mechanism.md) §DO ("act as if"); [snap-back-effect](./snap-back-effect.md) daily field-test discipline.

### Charity (compassion)

> "Treating everyone with respect is charity because it is not always, instantly, individually reciprocated. You cannot view it as transactional; instead you must see the big picture and act in this manner as a means of strengthening your own self-image, and as your contribution to society in general." (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 8)

Operationally: respect for others' worth, not because it's deserved or reciprocated, but because it's the operating mode of the success-personality. The self-image that respects others reciprocally respects itself; the self-image that treats others as worthless treats itself the same way.

**Wiki connection**: [GRACE](./grace-overview.md) (social-pragmatic encoder); [HEART](./heart-overview.md) (people-model encoder).

### Esteem

> "Self-doubt is insidious, and gnaws away at the self-image as cancer eats away at the body's organs." (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 8)

Operationally: the felt-sense that one (and others) are worthy of dignity and capable of growth. Distinct from self-confidence (which is a *task-specific* readiness). Esteem is *baseline*; self-confidence is *operational*. Esteem must come first; self-confidence builds on it.

**Wiki connection**: [self-image](./self-image.md) §audit; [spark-overview](./spark-overview.md) Trophy Palace (objective evidence of esteem-earned).

### Self-confidence

> "Creating a better self-image does not create new abilities, talents, powers; it releases and utilizes them." (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 8)

Operationally: task-specific felt readiness to perform. Built on **stored successes**, not on affirmation. The [Winning Feeling library](./winning-feeling.md) is the substrate. Pass-floor: can name 3+ past wins relevant to the current domain.

**Wiki connection**: [winning-feeling](./winning-feeling.md); [spark-overview](./spark-overview.md) Trophy Palace; [automaticity-and-reflex-training](./automaticity-and-reflex-training.md) reflex-card success-test ("Identify layer in <5 seconds").

### Self-acceptance

> "Instead of Self-Rejection, you must strive for Self-Acceptance. This means acknowledging that you are a unique, one-of-a-kind composite of strengths, weaknesses, knowledge and ignorance, experience and naivete, accomplishment and unrealized potential, and so is every other person." (source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 8)

Operationally: acknowledging the *actual* mix of one's life (strengths + weaknesses + knowledge + ignorance + accomplishment + unrealized-potential), without rejection or fantasy. Distinct from complacency: self-acceptance is the *substrate* on which growth happens; self-rejection prevents growth by refusing to accept the starting point.

**Wiki connection**: [self-image](./self-image.md) §Real personality (Ch 11 face); bible-state-of-the-dead (Hebrew holistic anthropology compatibility for Adventist readers).

## 30-day "act them out" protocol

(source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 7, end-of-chapter Prescription)

> "Act them out for thirty days. Experience them, and see if worry, guilt, hostility have not been diminished and if confidence has not been increased."

The protocol: pick one trait per day (rotating through the 7, with one day for review). For 30 days, deliberately *act as if* the trait were already operating. End-of-day reflection logs one moment where the trait fired.

**Pass-floor (METER metric)**: 30 days, 7 traits × ≥4 cycles, ≥1 moment logged per day.

This is a *behavioural* implementation of the same identity-rehearsal protocol that [theater-of-the-mind](./theater-of-the-mind.md) runs *cognitively*. The two compose: Theater builds the rehearsed identity in the mind; the 30-day act-out tests it in the field.

## Pairing with Adversity Quotient (Stoltz)

(source: pscyho-cybernetics-book-maxwell-maltz.pdf Ch 8, Stoltz mention)

Maltz cites Paul Stoltz's *Adversity Quotient* research (in-depth work with 100,000+ people across 100 companies since 1967) as compatible parallel science. Stoltz measures how people perceive challenges and how well they deal with them; high-AQ individuals show the same operational patterns as Maltz's Success Personality.

The wiki notes the convergence without claiming the two frameworks are identical — Stoltz's AQ is a measurable construct (CORE: Control, Ownership, Reach, Endurance); Maltz's S·U·C·C·E·S·S. is a memorable composite. They overlap but are not isomorphic.

## Failure modes

1. **Treating the 7 as innate "personality"** — they are acquirable. The page's whole point is the operational definitions and protocols.
2. **Drilling Self-confidence without Esteem** — task-confidence built on a foundation of self-doubt is brittle. Esteem must come first.
3. **Charity as transaction** — withholding respect until others earn it. Maltz: this is misunderstanding charity. The operating mode is unconditional; transactional respect is something else.
4. **Self-acceptance as complacency** — confusing "I accept where I am" with "I won't change." Self-acceptance is the *starting line*; growth runs from it.
5. **Skipping Understanding** — acting before reading the situation accurately. The ASM steers toward whatever target you supply; supply the wrong target, it pursues it faithfully.
6. **Courage drilled without Self-acceptance** — produces bravado, not courage. Bravado is brittle; courage survives error because it accepts the actor as flawed.

## Mnemonic and checksum

**Mnemonic**: the word **SUCCESS** spelled out on seven brass plaques along the Velvet-Aeon temple wall, in order — Sense of direction, Understanding, Courage, Charity, Esteem, Self-confidence, Self-acceptance — each lit by the single warm light overhead. Touch a plaque; the trait's operational definition surfaces.

**Checksum**: (a) recite the 7 letters in order under 6 s; (b) name one personal example of each from the last 7 days; (c) name the trait currently weakest in your operation and the practice you'll use to strengthen it. If all three answer cleanly, the mnemonic is operational.
