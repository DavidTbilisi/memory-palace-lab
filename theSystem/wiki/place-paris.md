## Description

- **Region**: Île-de-France, north-central France
- **Coordinates**: 48.8566° N, 2.3522° E
- **Elevation**: 35 m (city centre, near the Seine)
- **Climate band**: Oceanic (Köppen Cfb) — mild, wet, maritime-tempered
- **Drainage**: River Seine, flowing NW into the English Channel at Le Havre
- **Founded**: c. 250 BC as Lutetia, Parisii Gallic settlement on Île de la Cité

- Object is **load-bearing identity** (Eiffel = Paris uniquely, not just France)
- The hexagon-shape and Eiffel-shape are both **silhouette anchors** (per code-glyph-grammar freeze-on-choose discipline)

## Significance

1. **Political** — capital of France since 987 (Hugh Capet); seat of the National Assembly, Senate, and Élysée Palace.
2. **Cultural** — global anchor of Western art, fashion, philosophy; ~30M+ annual visitors pre-2020.
3. **Geographic** — natural river crossing on the Seine; Île de la Cité was the original defensible core.
