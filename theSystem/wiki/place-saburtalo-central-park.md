## Why this hub matters disproportionately

Green-space anchors are mnemonically privileged for two reasons:

1. **Sensory distinctiveness** — wind, leaves, smell, openness. Easier to fire the scene under fatigue.
2. **Real-world destination weight** — parks are common targets ("meet me at the park"). High recall-frequency = strong consolidation.

If you walk to the park weekly anyway, the hub maintains itself with zero drill overhead.
