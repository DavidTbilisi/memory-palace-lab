# SMASHIN' SCOPE

**Summary**: Tony Buzan's 12-principle memory protocol — Synaesthesia/Sensuality · Movement · Association · Sexuality · Humour · Imagination · Numbers · Symbolism · Colour · Order/Sequence · Positive Images · Exaggeration. External-canon ancestor of [remaps](./remaps.md); Buzan claimed Greek-mnemonic-tradition lineage for the list. Documented here for lineage clarity: REMAPS is the 12→6 compression of SMASHIN' SCOPE, with explicit absorption, reframing, or drop decisions for each of the original principles.

## What SMASHIN' SCOPE Is

SMASHIN' SCOPE is the mnemonic acronym Tony Buzan introduced to teach his 12 principles of memory enhancement. The list synthesizes the classical Greek *ars memoriae* (Simonides, the *Rhetorica ad Herennium*, the trained-imagination tradition) with mid-20th-century findings about hemispheric specialization (Sperry, the "left brain / right brain" framing Buzan popularized). The claim was that good natural mnemonists use *every* aspect of the mind, and the 12 letters of SMASHIN' SCOPE give a portable checklist for doing so.

Buzan is also the originator of [Mind Mapping](./external-problem-solving-frameworks.md) (cited in this wiki at external-problem-solving-frameworks N-ish). SMASHIN' SCOPE pre-dates that work and is the more memory-specific contribution.

## The Twelve Principles

### S — Synaesthesia / Sensuality

The blending of senses, and sensitisation across all of them. Buzan listed six sense-channels: vision · hearing · smell · taste · touch · **kinaesthesia** (awareness of bodily position and movement in space). Great natural memorisers, he claimed, train regularly across all six and learn to *blend* them — an image that is simultaneously seen, heard, smelled, and felt is more retrievable than one experienced through a single channel. (source: Memory Techniques Wiki)

### M — Movement

Static images are weakly retrievable. Movement gives the brain additional binding hooks. Buzan added two refinements: make movement **three-dimensional** (not flat motion-on-a-plane), and use **rhythms and variation of rhythm** within the moving image. Rhythmic variation, not steady motion, is what makes the image outstandingly memorable. (source: Memory Techniques Wiki)

### A — Association

Bind whatever you want to memorise to something stable already present in your mental environment — a palace locus, a familiar person, a drilled peg. Association is the link operation; without it, even a vivid image floats free with nothing to retrieve it by.

### S — Sexuality

"We all have a good memory in this area. Use it." Buzan's terse formulation; the implication is that sexually-charged imagery has high arousal and high salience, both of which encode strongly. (source: Memory Techniques Wiki)

### H — Humour

The funnier, more ridiculous, absurd, or surreal an image, the more memorable it is. Comic incongruity, slapstick, and the absurd activate attentional capture in ways that neutral imagery does not.

### I — Imagination

Buzan's "powerhouse" principle, framed with the Einstein quotation: *"Imagination is more important than knowledge. For knowledge is limited whereas imagination embraces the entire world, stimulating progress, giving birth to evolution."* (Memory Foundation) The more vividly you apply imagination to memory work, the better memory becomes. Imagination is the source of all the other principles' raw material.

### N — Numbers

Numbering adds specificity and efficiency to the **Order/Sequence** principle (see #10). Buzan treated it as a sub-principle of ordering — when you number items, you make order explicit and indexable.

### S — Symbolism

Substitute a more meaningful image for a normal, boring, or abstract concept. Use traditional symbols (stop sign, light bulb, scales of justice) when they compress meaning at a glance. Symbolic shorthand reduces the encoding cost for abstract material.

### C — Colour

Use the full range of the rainbow, ideally vibrant / highlighter colours. Coloured imagery is more memorable than monochrome. Buzan recommended *full saturation* as a default — not subtle colour, but vivid, deliberate colour assignment.

### O — Order and/or Sequence

Imposed structure (small-to-big, colour-grouping, category-sorting) provides "random access" — once you know the ordering rule, you can jump to any item by its index without traversing the whole list. Pairs with **N — Numbers**: ordering supplies the structure, numbering supplies the index.

### P — Positive Images

Positive and pleasant images outperform negative ones on average, Buzan claimed, *because the brain wants to return to the image*. A negative image, even if vivid and applying all the other principles, can be **blocked** by the brain's avoidance of unpleasant rehearsal. This is the most contested principle in the list (see "REMAPS Reframing" below).

### E — Exaggeration

In every image, exaggerate size (large or small), shape, or sound. Exaggerated features bypass perceptual ordinariness — the brain notices and re-rehearses what is *not* normal.

## Mapping to REMAPS (12 → 6 Compression)

[REMAPS](./remaps.md) is the Neural OS compression of SMASHIN' SCOPE from 12 principles to 6 moves. The compression is not lossless — some principles were absorbed, some were reframed, one was explicitly dropped. Mapping:

| SMASHIN' SCOPE principle | REMAPS slot | Compression decision |
|---|---|---|
| **S** — Synaesthesia / Sensuality | **S**ensations | Direct absorb. REMAPS Sensations is exactly this principle, with the aphantasia caveat added (see [memory-palace-for-aphantasia](./memory-palace-for-aphantasia.md)) for the ~26% multi-sensory subgroup who cannot fully engage this move. |
| **M** — Movement | **M**odify-Merge-**Move** | Direct absorb. REMAPS Move is exactly this principle. Rhythm-variation refinement preserved as a typical-application bullet. |
| **A** — Association | **A**ssociate | Direct absorb. REMAPS Associate is exactly this principle. |
| **S** — Sexuality | **A** — Aesthetic (sub-move) | Generalised. REMAPS folds Sexuality into the broader **Aesthetic** sub-move of Associate ("anything that captures attention beyond neutral"). The cultural-context-specificity of Buzan's principle is reduced in favour of a category that covers arousal, beauty, grotesqueness, and erotic salience together. |
| **H** — Humour | **P** — **P**lay (sub-move) | Direct absorb. REMAPS Play covers comic exaggeration, slapstick, absurdity. |
| **I** — Imagination | (no slot — prerequisite) | Reframed as prerequisite, not slot. REMAPS assumes imagination is the input substrate for all six moves; making it a separate move would be tautological. [visualization-training](./visualization-training.md) is the training-side companion that builds the substrate. |
| **N** — Numbers | (no slot — separation of concerns) | Dropped from REMAPS, owned elsewhere. Number-handling is delegated to [Major System](./major-system-for-mathematical-notation.md), [peg matrix](./peg-audio-visual-matrix.md), and the [UMTF](./universal-mental-tagging-framework.md) indexing tags. REMAPS is for *image transformation*; numbering is an *encoding layer* in its own right. |
| **S** — Symbolism | **S** — **S**ymbols (sub-move) | Direct absorb. REMAPS Sensations/Symbols is exactly this principle. |
| **C** — Colour | **S** — Sensations (visual sub-channel) + **E** — Exaggerate | Split-absorbed. Colour-as-vividness rides on Sensations; colour-as-distinctiveness rides on Exaggerate. REMAPS does not make Colour a top-level move because colour is one channel of sensory imagery, not an independent move. |
| **O** — Order / Sequence | **P** — **P**ath (sub-move) | Direct absorb. REMAPS Path covers spatial / temporal ordering through a route. |
| **P** — Positive Images | (not in REMAPS — actively dropped) | **Reframed**. REMAPS does *not* require positivity. The wiki explicitly uses grotesque, horror, and disgust imagery as retrieval hooks where appropriate (see the peg-matrix scenes, the Bible-book image prompts, and the [representation-rules](./representation-rules.md) "distinctive over pleasant" principle). REMAPS Aesthetic covers both beauty and grotesqueness. Buzan's avoidance-blocking argument is real but rare in practice; the wiki treats negative imagery as a powerful tool, not a hazard. |
| **E** — Exaggeration | **E**xaggerate | Direct absorb. REMAPS Exaggerate is exactly this principle, with Eliminate added as the strip-down companion (intensify the signal OR strip away the noise). |

## Why Compress 12 → 6

Three forces drove the compression:

1. **Working-memory ceiling.** A 12-item checklist exceeds the [7±2 working-memory](./working-memory.md) ceiling. A user trying to apply SMASHIN' SCOPE under load drops items silently. A 6-item checklist (REMAPS) fits within working memory, so the move-checklist *itself* survives rehearsal without leaking.

2. **Separation of concerns.** SMASHIN' SCOPE mixes *image-transformation* moves (Exaggerate, Movement, Synaesthesia, Symbolism, Humour, Colour, Sexuality, Association) with *indexing-strategy* concerns (Numbers, Order/Sequence) and *meta-prerequisites* (Imagination). REMAPS separates these: REMAPS is the image-transformation layer only. Indexing is owned by [peg-audio-visual-matrix](./peg-audio-visual-matrix.md), [major-system-for-mathematical-notation](./major-system-for-mathematical-notation.md), and the palace skeleton. Imagination is a prerequisite trained by [visualization-training](./visualization-training.md). Each concern gets its own page; REMAPS stays small.

3. **Operational discipline.** SMASHIN' SCOPE is a *checklist*; REMAPS is a *checklist with operational guidance*. REMAPS specifies *when* to apply (failed recall, bland scene, before-drilling check), *when not to* (first encoding pass, fluent material, pure structural problems), and *how many moves per revision pass* (2-3 dimensions, not all 6). SMASHIN' SCOPE has no such operational discipline — it is a list of dimensions to consider, not a protocol for revising encodings.

## What REMAPS Explicitly Drops

Two principles from SMASHIN' SCOPE are not present in REMAPS:

- **Imagination** as a slot — reframed as the input substrate that every move operates on. The training-side page is [visualization-training](./visualization-training.md) (8-stage drill ladder from near-zero to persistent inner world), with the [memory-palace-for-aphantasia](./memory-palace-for-aphantasia.md) companion for the imagery-deficient subgroup. Imagination doesn't need a slot in REMAPS because it doesn't *make* an image more memorable — it is the *raw material* the other six moves shape.

- **Positive Images** as a constraint — actively rejected. The wiki uses grotesque, horror, and disgust imagery where they encode strongly (see for instance the Bible-book seeds for Lamentations, Job, Ezekiel; the peg-matrix scenes that lean into impossibility and absurdity; the Tetris-glyph scenes that include explicit ⊥ contradiction and crashing motion). Buzan's positivity rule is treated here as a culturally calibrated preference, not a memory-optimal constraint. REMAPS Aesthetic explicitly covers "beauty or grotesqueness; anything that captures attention beyond neutral."

## Lineage

The 12 principles in SMASHIN' SCOPE are not Buzan's original invention — they are his synthesis of:

- The classical Greek *ars memoriae* (Simonides of Ceos, *Rhetorica ad Herennium*, Quintilian) — the trained-imagination + locus-method tradition. Movement, Association, Exaggeration, and Symbolism all have antecedents in *Ad Herennium*'s discussion of *imagines agentes* (active images).
- 20th-century imagery research (Paivio's dual-coding theory 1971 — see [remaps](./remaps.md) §"External grounding") — Colour and Synaesthesia.
- Mid-century hemispheric-specialization findings (Sperry) — Buzan's stated rationale for mixing left-cortex (Numbers, Order) and right-cortex (Colour, Imagination, Sexuality) channels in one protocol.

Buzan's contribution was the **acronym** — making the 12-principle list portable and rehearsable. REMAPS's contribution is the **compression** — making the working-memory-fitting subset and adding operational discipline.
