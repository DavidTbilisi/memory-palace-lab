# SEM3 Ordinal Song — *The Gate and the Vine*

**Summary**: The companion carrier to [*Ten Kinds of Air*](./sem3-song.md), covering the twenty cells that song deliberately walked past — [SEM3](./sem3.md)'s two ordinal bands, 8 Rainbow and 9 Solar system. Both bands ride as **bare ordered sequences with only their zero named**, because once the band's own order is installed the address is the position — the digits are counted off, not sung. The two tracks partition SEM3 by derivability: the first sings the generator and refuses the eighty derivable cells, this one sings the twenty the rule cannot reach.

## Why it exists

[sem3](./sem3.md) says the twenty ordinal cells "cost nothing — provided the exception itself is installed." True, and incomplete. There is a second exception nobody had registered, and it is where the confident wrong answers come from:

**The band is not the list you already learned.**

- [cultural-string-sequences](./cultural-string-sequences.md) §1 registers the rainbow as **ROYGBIV, seven letters**. SEM3's band 8 runs to **ten**, completing the spectrum with three achromatics — Black, Gray, White.
- [cultural-string-sequences](./cultural-string-sequences.md) §4 registers the planets as **Mercury→Neptune, eight, Pluto excluded post-2006**. SEM3's band 9 runs to **ten**: it takes the **Sun at 0** and **keeps Pluto at 9**.

Neither page is wrong — they are different constructs. A cultural sequence answers *what comes next*; a SEM3 band is a ten-slot peg index that must be exactly full. But someone reaching for the sequence they know is **off by one and short by two**, and gets a confident answer either way. That is the payload here.

## Design decisions (locked 2026-09-07)

- **Open litany, and here the cells *are* the payload.** [sem3-song](./sem3-song.md) refused to sing cells because the first-consonant rule generates them. These twenty are the only cells in SEM3 the rule does not reach — it is revoked in both bands — so nothing generates them and they must be carried. Same doctrine, opposite conclusion.
- **The two tracks partition SEM3 by derivability.** Eighty generated cells belong to the rule and are sung nowhere; twenty ungenerated cells belong here. No overlap, no gap — which is a checksum on the cycle, not just on either song.
- **Sequence, not counting — corrected 2026-09-07 (David's call).** The first render chanted a digit against every cell (*Orange, one. Yellow, two.*) on the argument that ROYGBIV gives order and the band needs address. That argument is wrong, and wrong in the same way this cycle exists to prevent: **once the band's own sequence is in order, the address *is* the position**, so a spoken digit is derivable output — the table anti-pattern of [sem3](./sem3.md) §The table is not the content, wearing a counting song. What is genuinely not derivable is the sequence's two **extensions** (past violet, and the Sun in front) and the fact that it **starts at zero**. So the verses carry the bare sequence, and exactly one number survives per band: the zero, named because it is the only index a counter would get wrong. Everything after it is counted, not sung.
- **Both phrase-breaks sit exactly on a divergence.** Band 8 breaks **7 + 3**, whispered, after Violet — where the cultural sequence ends and the band keeps going. Band 9 breaks **1 + 9** after the Sun — where the cultural sequence *starts* and the band already spent a cell. Phrase it correctly and the classic error is unavailable.
- **The off-by-one is named twice, once per band.** *Red. And red is zero, not one.* / *The sun is not a planet. The sun is zero.* The owner page states the orders; it does not state that both bands begin at a place a counter would not.
- **Arrangement inverts [sem3-song](./sem3-song.md).** There the plucked figure never moved and the drones re-coloured — the prop fixed, the air changing. Here there **is no air**: bands 8 and 9 have no sensory letter. So the drones go still and the plucked figure **climbs a ten-step ladder, one step per cell**, pitch standing in for digit. The two tracks swap which element moves, and the swap is the exception rendered as sound.
- **One escalation, on Pluto.** *They took it off the list. The vine keeps it.* The catalogue's register is loss (music-profile: something that was there and is now gone); this peak is a thing struck off a list and kept anyway, which is the same register inverted. It is also the single highest-value trap in the song.
- **A cycle, not a sequel.** Same room, same table, same dial, same English folk profile, same female alto, same beatless constraint — per the `/suno` rule that a concept viewed from several angles shares one scene and one MASTER profile, because the sonic sameness is the argument. The climbing ladder is the one deliberate difference and it is doctrinal.
- **The two sequences are recited here, and that is not a reversal.** [sem3-song](./sem3-song.md) declined to recite them because its subject was the ten categories and the sequences were free *there*. This song's subject **is** the twenty cells, and what it actually installs is the digit on each — which is not free anywhere.
- **No new [METER](./meter-overview.md) namespace.** Inherited from [sem3](./sem3.md) §Measurement's closed spine, as [sem3-song](./sem3-song.md) does.

## The song (Suno-ready)

```
[Intro]
[Sung, low and sustained, very close] [low drone, no pulse]
Same room. Same table. Same thing on it.
I turned past seven.
Two stations left. Neither one speaks.

[Verse 1]
[Sung, each name one scale step higher than the last] [plucked ladder climbing with the voice]
Red is where it starts. Red is zero.
Orange, yellow, green,
blue, indigo, violet.
[Sung very quietly]
There the rainbow ends.
[Sung, the ornament gone, plainer]
Black. Gray. White.

[Chorus]
[Sung, deep and open, sustained vowels]
Nothing here is lettered.
Count it. Don't spell it.
The gate and the vine.
Ten each, and no letters at all.

[Verse 2]
[Sung, the ladder climbing again from below]
The sun is not a planet.
The sun is zero.
Mercury, Venus, Earth,
Mars, Jupiter, Saturn,
Uranus, Neptune.
[Crescendo]
And Pluto.
They took it off the list.
The vine keeps it.

[Chorus]
Nothing here is lettered.
Count it. Don't spell it.
The gate and the vine.
Ten each, and no letters at all.

[Bridge]
[Sung, close, the line rising]
Ask the gate for a letter. It lies.
Ask the vine. It lies too.
The sun says S, and it means nothing.
Every other band, the sound is the number.
Not here. Not these two.
[Sung very quietly]
Whatever list you learned outside -
it is not this list.
Seven colours out there. Ten in the gate.
Eight planets out there. Ten on the vine.

[Chorus]
[Sung full, low choir underneath]
Nothing here is lettered.
Count it. Don't spell it.
The gate and the vine.
Ten each, and no letters at all.

[Outro]
[Sung, fading with the drone]
Eight bands say their own names.
Two bands only stand in line.
Count from the red. Count from the sun.
Never from one.
Turn the dial back. It's still there.
```

## Line-to-payload map — twelve members

Two sequences, their two zero-anchors and their two extensions, then six rule members. The row count is the completeness check.

| # | Lyric | Payload member |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Red is where it starts. Red is zero.* | band 8's zero-index — the one digit position cannot supply |
| 2 | *Orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet* | `81`–`86`, carried as sequence; the digits fall out of position |
| 3 | *There the rainbow ends* (whispered, at the 7 + 3 break) | ROYGBIV supplies only `80`–`86` — the cultural sequence stops here and the band does not |
| 4 | *Black. Gray. White.* | `87`–`89` — the three achromatics that complete the band, in order |
| 5 | *The sun is not a planet. The sun is zero.* | band 9's zero-index, and the off-by-one that follows from the Sun occupying a cell |
| 6 | *Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune* | `91`–`98`, carried as sequence |
| 7 | *And Pluto. They took it off the list. The vine keeps it.* | `99` — Pluto retained, because the band needs ten slots; **not** [cultural-string-sequences](./cultural-string-sequences.md) §4's post-2006 eight |
| 8 | *Ask the gate for a letter. It lies. Ask the vine. It lies too.* | the first-consonant rule is revoked in both bands |
| 9 | *Every other band, the sound is the number. Not here. Not these two.* | the exception is exactly two categories wide — the boundary, stated |
| 10 | *The sun says S, and it means nothing* | `9000` Sun satisfies S = 0 by coincidence; [sem3](./sem3.md) warns explicitly against generalising from it |
| 11 | *Whatever list you learned outside — it is not this list. Seven colours out there, ten in the gate. Eight planets out there, ten on the vine.* | the band is not the cultural sequence; both divergences with their counts |
| 12 | *Count from the red. Count from the sun. Never from one.* | both bands are zero-indexed — the shared off-by-one, closed in one line |

The twenty cells are **two rows, not twenty**: they ride as ordered sequences, and their addresses are counted off position rather than sung. That collapse is the correction of 2026-09-07 and the reason the row count fell from 27 to 12.

## Render spec (MASTER)

| Slot | Value |
|---|---|
| **M** Meter | ~58 BPM, slow and grave, **completely beatless**; the pulse is a fingerpicked mountain-dulcimer figure that **climbs one step per name**, a rising ten-note ladder, and the sung melody rises with it |
| **A** Arrangement | one deep female alto in a low contralto register; **a fingerpicked mountain-dulcimer figure climbing one step per name**; hurdy-gurdy, fiddle and Northumbrian smallpipes **drones held completely still** beneath it; English concertina held long, wooden flute; low women's close-harmony chorus on the final chorus |
| **S** Space/mix | voice close and dry against cavernous stone reverb; the room does not change — only the ladder moves |
| **T** Timbre | **fully sung throughout** — a clear rising melody, each name a scale step higher than the last, every word carried on pitch; *in* scale, microtonal inflection; wordless vocalise; whispered doubling at the 7 + 3 break; temple bowls as sustained decay, never a pulse |
| **E** Energy-arc | Mode B plateau, chorus identical all three times; one `[Crescendo]` on *And Pluto. Pluto, nine.* |
| **R** Restrict | no drums, percussion, beat, rhythm section, 808, hi-hats, bodhran, tabor or tambourine; **and nothing merry** — jig, reel, hornpipe, sea shanty, pub singalong, which is English trad's own way of failing the darkness gate; no new-age or celtic-new-age reading; no spoken word, recitation or narration; no pop polish, belted vocal, rap, autotune |

**Style prompt:**

```
English traditional folk, in the unaccompanied Child-ballad manner, slow and grave, completely beatless with no drums or percussion, deep female alto voice in a low contralto register, close and very clear, fully sung throughout with a strong sustained melody and long legato lines, every word carried on pitch, Dorian and Aeolian modal harmony, hurdy-gurdy and fiddle drones, Northumbrian smallpipes drone, English concertina held long, wooden flute, a low women's close-harmony chorus in open fifths, wordless vocalise beneath the words, cavernous stone-church reverb, tape warmth, ancient and severe and grief-stricken, a supernatural ballad and never a merry one, around 58 BPM, the pulse carried by a fingerpicked mountain-dulcimer figure that climbs one step higher on every name in a rising ten-note ladder, the drones held completely still beneath it, the sung melody itself rising a scale step on each name
```

**Exclude:** `drums, percussion, beat, rhythm section, 808, hi-hats, bodhran, tabor, tambourine, trap, new age, spa, relaxation, wellness ambient, celtic new age, upbeat, cheerful, jaunty, jig, reel, hornpipe, sea shanty, pub singalong, drinking song, pop, EDM, bright synths, belted vocals, rap, autotune, spoken word, spoken vocals, recitation, narration, monologue, talking, whispering`

**The one thing to listen for.** The climbing ladder is an *instruction*, not a guarantee — Suno is not given pitch control, and a render that flattens the ladder into a static figure has lost the arrangement's argument while keeping every word. That is a listen-gate question and cannot be settled from the lyric sheet.

## Falsifier

Two weeks cold, **before opening [sem3](./sem3.md) or [cultural-string-sequences](./cultural-string-sequences.md)**: write out both bands with digits attached, then answer three probes — *what is `84`? what is `97`? what is at `99`?*

Pass = twenty cells correctly addressed, both bands starting at zero, and Pluto present. Four failures it separates:

1. **A sequence returns but cannot be counted off.** The order installed without its zero-anchor, so `84` comes back as Indigo — the count started at one.
2. **Band 9 comes back one short and shifted.** The cultural eight-planet sequence won; `93` returns Mars instead of Earth. The single most likely failure.
3. **Band 8 stops at Violet.** ROYGBIV won; the three achromatics were never installed as part of the band.
4. **A letter is offered for a cell.** The revocation did not take, which is the failure [sem3-song](./sem3-song.md)'s bridge is about and this song's chorus repeats three times.

Measured through the existing instrumentation per [sem3](./sem3.md) §Measurement — Anki decks `001 Memory::015 Beyond 100 (SEM3)` and `017 SEM3 + Major`, whose reviews reach METER through `tools/meter-anki-addon`. No new event.

## Mnemonic

*Two stations at the end of the dial, and neither one speaks.* Everywhere else on the dial a thing says its own number as it arrives. Not here. At the gate the colours run in their own order and keep going past the rainbow into black, gray, white. At the vine you count worlds and the counting starts at the sun, which is not a world at all, and ends at the one they struck off the list and the vine kept anyway. Both begin at zero. Neither begins at one.

## Checksum

1. Both bands break their phrasing exactly once. Where, and what does each break mark?
2. The other song refused to sing cells; this one sings twenty. What is the single rule that makes both decisions the same decision?
3. What does the plucked figure do here that it is forbidden from doing in [sem3-song](./sem3-song.md), and which payload claim decides that?
4. Someone recites the planets correctly and still gets `93` wrong. What did they reach for?
