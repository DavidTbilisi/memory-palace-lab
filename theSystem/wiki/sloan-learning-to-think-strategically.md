# Learning to Think Strategically — Julia Sloan

**Summary**: Source-summary page for Julia Sloan, *Learning to Think Strategically* (4th ed., Routledge 2020, 327 pp). The book grounds strategic thinking in Habermas's critical theory and Mezirow's adult-learning theory, arguing that strategic thinking is a **distinct cognitive cluster from strategic planning**, learned **informally** through experience, prior successful life experience, dialogue, and the arts. Source for 12 concept pages downstream and the **AND polarity thinking** primitive newly registered in the wiki.

**Sources**:

## What's in the book

Eight parts, eighteen chapters. The spine:

1. **Parts I–II** (history + definition) — Western strategy's shift from ancient Greek *metos* (oscillation between cosmos and chaos) to twentieth-century technical rationality; the design vs emergent schools (Ansoff vs Mintzberg); Sloan's own definition: *strategic thinking is a problem-focused, intent-driven approach based on critical theory, supported by a complex cluster of cognitive capabilities distinct from strategic planning*. The **Sloan Triad Model** (Thinking ↔ Planning ↔ Implementation) introduced as the operating frame.
2. **Part III** (foundation) — Framing as a structural process; the 6 framing traps; the surf/dive learning domains (Mezirow's instrumental/communicative); content/process/premise reflection.
3. **Part IV** (testing) — Critical dialogue as informed/risky/critical; dialogue vs discussion vs debate; the 9-question dive drill; the 7 strategic-thinking dimensions; the AAR (After-Action Review) from the US Army.
4. **Part V** (intuition) — Tacit knowledge (Schon); Klein's recognition-primed decision (RPD); reflection-in-action vs reflection-on-action; the element of surprise; intuition as a check on analysis (and vice versa).
5. **Part VI** (culture) — Culture is not a primary factor — informal learning supersedes culture. The hierarchy dimension. The **5 universal attributes** of strategic thinkers: Imagination · Broad perspective · Juggle · No-control-over · Desire-to-win.
6. **Part VII** (arts) — Engaging in the arts as the primary informal-learning vehicle for the 5 attributes. The **three unconventional approaches**: AND polarity thinking · Metaphor (especially complex conceptual metaphor) · Drawing. Plus three conventional approaches: Action Learning (Revans), Lateral Thinking (De Bono), Learning Agility.
7. **Part VIII** (suggestions) — A "cheat sheet" for individuals + organizations.

### Unlocks surfaced

1. **AND polarity × Five Elements (Aether)** — AND polarity is the operational primitive for the Aether/Strategic element of [automaticity-and-reflex-training](./automaticity-and-reflex-training.md); previously the Aether cell was named but lacked a concrete operational form.
2. **Surf/dive × Lamp/Scale/Sword** — the two are *orthogonal* axes. Lamp/Scale/Sword measures pressure-of-execution; surf/dive measures depth-of-reflection. Together they form a 2×2 the wiki was missing.
3. **6 framing traps × MBTI typology** — Know-it-alls/Experts/Satisfieds/Conformists are personality-tied; the traps map onto MBTI function-stack inferior-function takeovers (see mbti-shadow-and-stress).
4. **AND polarity + Metaphor + Drawing trio** — Sloan's empirical finding (~3-year strategy-memory retention when the trio is used together) is the strongest external validation yet for the wiki's REMAPS + visual-encoding doctrine.
5. **Critical theory (Habermas) as philosophical grounding** — Sloan grounds the whole approach in Habermas's communicative reason. The wiki had not previously cited critical theory; this fills a citation gap for the dialogue/inquiry layer.
6. **AAR as sister to [arc-framework](./arc-framework.md) Close** — Army's 5-question protocol is a structured instance of the wiki's Close phase, importable directly.

## Where Sloan's framing differs from the wiki

- **"Strategic thinking is not teachable, only learnable"** — Sloan's strong position. The wiki disagrees with the strong form: it teaches via [problem-solving-os](./problem-solving-os.md), drill ladders, and Encoded SR. But the spirit is right — *no formula*, the learner must build the experience base.
- **Arts as the primary learning vehicle** — Sloan repeats this often. The wiki treats arts as one substrate among several (palace work, drill, dialogue, journaling). Sloan's emphasis is welcome and integrated, but not promoted to exclusive status.
- **No formal METER analog in Sloan** — Sloan resists measurement ("Avoid an obsession with measuring everything"). The wiki disagrees: [METER](./meter-overview.md) is load-bearing. Sloan's caution is preserved as a guardrail (over-measurement of the affective layer collapses it), not as a measurement veto.

## Mnemonic

A samurai (Musashi) and a Bedouin elder (Sheikh Zayed) sit cross-legged on opposite sides of an **AND polarity** drawn as a tug-of-rope between a **left fist** (planning, convergent, control) and a **right fist** (thinking, divergent, surrender). Between the two fists rises a **third figure** — a woman with long hair lifted by wind, holding a **lit lamp** in one hand and a **paintbrush** in the other (the arts; imagination). She is **fragile** in the face but **strong** in the stance per feedback_image_face_and_hair; her gown is **draped in 5 sashes** (the 5 attributes), each a different color: **imagination gold**, **broad-perspective silver**, **juggle green** (with 7 spinning plates above her head, one per [seven-strategic-thinking-dimensions](./seven-strategic-thinking-dimensions.md) dimension), **no-control violet** (loose ribbon trailing into a storm), **desire-to-win crimson** (sash knotted tight). Six small **stone obstacles** (the 6 framing traps) line the path behind her, each carved with a face — the immediate-gratifier sneer, the know-it-all smirk, the expert squint, the satisfied yawn, the conformist mask, the over-simplifier shrug. Above, the sky is split into **surf clouds** (light, surface) and a **dive ocean** (deep, dark blue) — the sky and ocean meet at a hard horizon, the cluster boundary. Velvet Aeon sub-mode: **sorrow-as-guidance** — the woman walks because the rope between the two fists is unresolvable, and the resolution is the walking.
