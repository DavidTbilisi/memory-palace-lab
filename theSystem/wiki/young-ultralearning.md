# Ultralearning (Young)

**Summary**: *Ultralearning: Master Hard Skills, Outsmart the Competition, and Accelerate Your Career* (Scott H. Young, HarperCollins/Thorsons 2019, ~250 pp) is the wiki's **T2 method framework** for self-directed learning *projects* — aggressive, self-designed campaigns run outside institutions. Its contribution is not new science but **project architecture**: it takes principles the T1 spine ([brown-make-it-stick](./brown-make-it-stick.md), [ericsson-peak](./ericsson-peak.md), [willingham-cognitive-principles](./willingham-cognitive-principles.md)) established in the lab and arranges them into a sequence a solo learner can actually run, from scoping through retention. The book states nine principles — **[Metalearning](./metalearning.md) · Focus · [Directness](./directness-principle.md) · Drill · Retrieval · Feedback · Retention · Intuition · Experimentation**. This page is the source-summary / routing owner; the two principles the wiki adopted as owned concepts are defined on their own pages, and most of the rest route to owners that already existed.

## What the book is, and what tier it sits at

The 12-book ingest classified this source **T2 — method framework**, and the distinction is load-bearing. T1 sources ([brown-make-it-stick](./brown-make-it-stick.md), [ericsson-peak](./ericsson-peak.md), [willingham-cognitive-principles](./willingham-cognitive-principles.md)) are the wiki's *citation backstop*: peer-reviewed findings it can lean on. Young is a **practitioner synthesis** — he did not run the studies, and his evidence is his own campaigns plus the research he is summarising. So the wiki cites Young for **project structure** and cites the T1 spine for **why the components work**.

That split is why almost every principle below routes somewhere else. Ultralearning's real contribution is the *ordering and packaging*, not the parts.

## The nine principles — and where each one lives

Nine members, which is past the point where a shape helps: [representation-rules](./representation-rules.md) Rule 10 caps count-shapes at 7 and prescribes an **ordered ladder** above that, which is also how the book presents them — a project runs roughly top to bottom.

| # | Principle | What it asks | Wiki owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Metalearning** | Map the territory before starting; Why-What-How; ~10% of project time | [metalearning](./metalearning.md) |
| 2 | **Focus** | Start, sustain, and sharpen attention; defeat procrastination and shallow work | *no owner* — nearest is [PULSE](./pulse-overview.md) (state gating) |
| 3 | **Directness** | Practise in the context of use; minimise proxies and detours | [directness-principle](./directness-principle.md) |
| 4 | **Drill** | Attack the rate-limiting sub-skill in isolation | [pattern-drilling](./pattern-drilling.md) · [drill-generator](./drill-generator.md) · [making-smaller-circles](./making-smaller-circles.md) |
| 5 | **Retrieval** | Test yourself rather than review; produce from memory | [active-recall](./active-recall.md) |
| 6 | **Feedback** | Seek it aggressively; separate the useful signal from the noise | *no single owner* — feedback-loop-taxonomy is the structural parent |
| 7 | **Retention** | Design against forgetting rather than discovering it later | [spaced-repetition](./spaced-repetition.md) |
| 8 | **Intuition** | Build deep understanding, not memorised procedure; the Feynman technique | [self-explanation](./self-explanation.md) · [understand-deeply-habit](./understand-deeply-habit.md) |
| 9 | **Experimentation** | Once competent, deliberately vary method and style | [transformative-change-habit](./transformative-change-habit.md) |

Two of the nine are the wiki's own owned concepts (1 and 3); five route to owners that already existed; **two — Focus and Feedback — have no dedicated owner page**, which is a real gap this reconstruction surfaces rather than papers over.

## What it adds that the T1 spine does not

The T1 sources tell you what works in a study session. Young's addition is the **project envelope around the sessions** — the questions that come *before* any technique choice and *after* the last one:

- **Scoping.** [metalearning](./metalearning.md) is the front end nothing else in the canon supplies: what exactly is the skill, what does competence look like, what resources exist, and how will the campaign be structured. Spending ~10% of the project on this is a claim about *allocation*, not about learning mechanism.
- **Sequencing.** Drill (4) is placed after directness (3) deliberately: isolate a sub-skill only once you know the real performance it feeds, or you drill the wrong thing efficiently. That ordering constraint is the book's most transferable structural idea.
- **Exit.** Retention (7) and experimentation (9) treat the *end* of a project as designed rather than assumed — the canon's usual failure is stopping at "learned" with no plan for decay or for moving past competence.

## How it sits in Neural OS

Ultralearning is closest in kind to the wiki's own **operating stacks** — it is a rhythm over existing components rather than a new encoder. The [directness-principle](./directness-principle.md) is the piece with the widest reach, because the wiki keeps re-deriving it: [soft-zone-vs-hard-zone](./soft-zone-vs-hard-zone.md) is directness applied to attentional conditions, and the [language-learning-protocol](./language-learning-protocol.md) is directness applied to L2. [metalearning](./metalearning.md) is the piece with the least competition — nothing else in the canon owns the pre-project mapping step.

The registered unlock from this ingest pairs [directness-principle](./directness-principle.md) with [Brown](./brown-make-it-stick.md) and [Ericsson](./ericsson-peak.md): **transfer is engineered, not assumed** (see [composability-index](./composability-index.md)).

## Mnemonic

**ULTRA** — the nine principles compressed to the five decisions a campaign actually makes: **U**nderstand the territory first (1) · **L**ock attention (2) · **T**rain in the real context (3, 4) · **R**etrieve rather than review (5, 7) · **A**djust from feedback and variation (6, 8, 9). If a learning project skipped **U**, it is optimising a route it never mapped.
