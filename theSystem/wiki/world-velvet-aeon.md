## What Velvet Aeon Is

A world where the universe never stopped being haunted.

The deserts are endless and pale gold, but they are not empty. Colossal clocktowers rise from the dunes like abandoned gods, gears still turning long after civilization disappeared. Time is physical there — drifting through the air as glowing ribbons, collecting in ruined cemeteries, trapped inside old bells and broken mirrors. Travelers move slowly because memory itself has weight.

Every city was built around grief. Cathedrals stand half-buried beneath red moons; marble angels erode into faceless silhouettes; gardens overgrow tombs until flowers and bones become indistinguishable. Children leave offerings for things older than humanity — cups of tea, wilted lilies, tiny silver keys — and the ancient beings accept them gently.

The supernatural is not chaotic or loud. It is intimate. Cosmic entities lean through cracked wooden ceilings to speak softly with lonely people. Star-filled gods wander the dunes wrapped in white cloth, carrying entire galaxies beneath their robes like lantern light. They are terrifying only because they are vast and sad and eternal. They remember extinct kingdoms the way humans remember childhood homes.

Beauty and dread are inseparable. Queens wear crowns of black thorned metal that climb over skin like living lace. Palaces glow crimson from eclipsed moons. Everything is ceremonial: mourning, love, war, memory. Even silence feels choreographed. Even decay is elegant.

The people are small but never meaningless. The orphan girl in a cemetery is as important as the celestial titan above her. A lone traveler facing a clocktower can alter history simply by remembering something everyone else forgot. Human fragility is sacred because it fades. The gods envy mortals for their endings.

The sky is always enormous. Nebulas drift visibly overhead at night; constellations move like living creatures; sometimes the stars gather into humanoid forms and watch the earth in silence; sometimes moons bleed red for weeks at a time and everyone speaks softer until the colour fades.

Nothing is modern in spirit, even when impossible. It feels painted rather than constructed — Delacroix emotion, Ingres divinity, and dreamlike cosmic horror fused — but the *medium* is photoreal. Large-format film grain. Velvet shadows. Gold dust in the air. Candlelight against infinite darkness. Vast spaces broken by singular moments of tenderness.

A civilization built on **sacred memory · cosmic loneliness · feminine divinity · romantic ruin · sorrow as beauty as guidance**.

## The Five Thematic Preserves

Every Velvet Aeon image must honor *at least one* of these five themes — they are the "Preserve" half of the [CLAMP P slot](./clamp-render-lens.md) for this world, restated on every prompt:

1. **Sacred memory** — something is being remembered, mourned, or kept. The image is an act of remembrance.
2. **Cosmic loneliness** — the subject is alone against a vast indifferent / patient cosmos; smallness is the point.
3. **Feminine divinity** — divinity in this world slants feminine: queens, female cosmic entities, mother-figures, mourning-bride figures. Not exclusive, but default.
4. **Romantic ruin** — decay is elegant; erosion reveals beauty rather than destroying it; the ruin is loved.
5. **Sorrow as guidance** — grief is not endpoint but compass; the figures are guided by what they have lost.

## The Four Sub-Modes

Velvet Aeon has four operational sub-modes that share substrate but differ in subject scale, function, and CLAMP M·L·C·A parameters. Three are for memory-encoding scenes (Mortal, Cosmic, Environment); one is for identity-rehearsal scenes (Identity, added 2026-05-24 from the [Psycho-Cybernetics](./psycho-cybernetics-maltz.md) ingest).

### Mode · Mortal

**Subject**: small humans at threshold moments. The orphan girl in a cemetery. A lone traveler at a clocktower. A child leaving an offering at a roadside shrine. An old man at a half-buried cathedral. A widow at a tomb-garden. Always one figure, alone, treated with reverence not pity.

**CLAMP defaults**:
- **C** — Medium-format film look (Hasselblad / Mamiya 7); 50–85mm portrait compression; shallow depth of field; figure 40–70% of frame; subject sharp, atmosphere falling away
- **L** — Candlelight against infinite darkness · single warm lantern + suspended gold dust + deep velvet shadow · OR distant nebula glow with foreground in candle-light
- **A** — `1024×1536` HD portrait
- **M** — *Photoreal large-format film, Avedon-level resolution carrying Delacroix emotional register, Gregory Crewdson staged narrative photography, Roger Deakins lighting, Tarkovsky patience*
- **P** — Preserve: face visible OR full silhouette (choose one and commit); single subject; one symbolic object present (offering, key, lily, bell, mirror, candle); pre-modern dress

### Mode · Cosmic

**Subject**: original divine entities — sublime, intimate, sad. The white-cloth star-filled god leaning through a cracked wooden ceiling. The queen with crown of black thorned metal grown into her skin. The eroded faceless marble angel turning slowly in moonlight. A celestial titan with galaxies visible beneath the robes like lantern light. Always one entity; never named pantheon (no Zeus, no Christian-iconography angels, no Norse/Egyptian/etc).

**CLAMP defaults**:
- **C** — 35–85mm; figure fills 50–70% of frame; vast negative space above and around to communicate cosmic scale
- **L** — Cosmic nebula glow (distant) + candlelight foreground · OR red-moon ambient wash (ceremonial, used when a red-moon scene is established) · OR self-illumination radiating through cloth/skin
- **A** — `1024×1536` HD portrait
- **M** — *Photoreal large-format film with Delacroix-emotional staging, Deakins × Crewdson × Dune visual register, painterly soul photoreal capture*
- **P** — Preserve: cosmic vastness visible *through* the entity (galaxies behind cloth, stars in robe, nebula behind shoulder); intimate scale despite cosmic identity (the god is leaning down, not towering); divinity reads sad/eternal/intimate, never wrathful or powerful

### Mode · Environment

**Subject**: wide cosmic-mournful landscapes. The dune-clocktower with gears still turning. The half-buried cathedral under a red moon. The tomb-garden where flowers and bones merge. A nebula sky above a sleeping city. A ruined cemetery at dusk. Human figures if present are *tiny against scale*.

**CLAMP defaults**:
- **C** — 24–35mm wide angle; anamorphic 2.39:1 or landscape 4:5 framing; deep depth of field; patient stillness
- **L** — Red-moon ambient wash · distant nebula glow above ruin · gold-dust-suspended-in-air at low backlit angle · pre-dawn candle-lantern village in vast desert dark
- **A** — `1536×1024` HD landscape (default) OR `2560×1440` 2K for premium establishing
- **M** — *Deakins establishing shot × Dune atmospheric scale × Tarkovsky long-take patience × Crewdson staged tableau*, photoreal large-format film
- **P** — Preserve: pre-modern architecture (no glass towers, no fluorescent light, no modern grid layouts); decay visible and elegant; sky huge; human figures (if any) are tiny against the scale of the world

### Mode · Identity (added 2026-05-24 from Psycho-Cybernetics ingest)

**Subject**: the practitioner *as the kind of person they are becoming*, captured mid-operation — not in glory, not at the moment of triumph, but in the steady operation of the rehearsed identity. This sub-mode supports [Theater of the Mind](./theater-of-the-mind.md) rehearsals and SPARK Trophy-locus art. The figure is **the practitioner** (self-image avatar), not a third-party character.

**Functional difference from Mortal/Cosmic/Environment**: those sub-modes render *scenes for memory encoding*. Identity sub-mode renders *scenes for self-image re-programming*. Different target, different preserve discipline.

**CLAMP defaults**:
- **C** — First-person or close-third (3/4 over-shoulder); never far long-shot. Identity is *inhabited*, not observed from a distance. 35–50mm; eye-level or slightly below; medium-shallow depth-of-field; sense of *being in the body*, not looking at it
- **L** — Single warm light source (window-light, lamp, candle, dawn-through-curtain). No chaos, no storms, no anxiety atmosphere. The calmness of the lighting is load-bearing — it primes the [winning feeling](./winning-feeling.md)'s somatic register
- **A** — Standard Mortal sub-mode aspect by default (`1024×1536` portrait or `1024×1024` square); Cosmic aspect (`2:3` epic portrait) only for vocation-scale identity scenes (acceptance of role, ordination, peak-public-performance)
- **M** — Velvet Aeon canonical photoreal-with-painterly-soul; lean Ingres-portrait + Crewdson interior over Delacroix-drama; calm composition, no high-stakes action
- **P** — **Preserve required**: *sacred memory* + *feminine divinity* (the self-image as the divine portrait being polished). **Preserve forbidden**: *romantic ruin* — primes the failure mechanism; the rubber band yanks toward decay. *Cosmic loneliness* allowed only when rehearsing identity in solitude (writing, prayer, study). *Sorrow as guidance* allowed only when rehearsing a recovery / forgiveness identity scene; never the dominant note

**Figure rules** (extending [figure feedback](./feedback-image-figure-rule.md) and face/hair feedback):
- The practitioner's face uses the **STRONG archetype** (angular jaw, piercing gaze) when rehearsing ASM-active identities — public performance, contested negotiation, leadership, decisive action
- The **FRAGILE archetype** (luminous or porcelain) when rehearsing the relax-substrate identity — quiet creativity, study, prayer, repair from setback
- Both archetypes are allowed in the same identity if the rehearsal alternates modes (e.g., a leader who is both decisive in public and quiet in study)
- Hair: long and shining (waist+); the water/dissolution effect from feedback applies sparingly — used to mark *transitions* between identity states, not the default identity

**Subject discipline — operation-in-progress, not outcome**:
- **Yes**: yourself mid-talk, fielding a question; mid-writing at the desk; mid-conversation across a table; mid-workout in the studio; mid-prayer; mid-decision at the threshold of a difficult choice
- **No**: yourself receiving applause; yourself holding the trophy; yourself promoted; yourself published. Outcome scenes don't update the self-image — they record the past or fantasise the future. Identity sub-mode renders the *operation*, which is what the brain registers as experience.

**Worked example invocation**:

```
Velvet Aeon | Mode = Identity | Subject = practitioner (David) mid-talk to ~30
people in a small lecture hall, fielding a question at minute 24 with calm
focus | C: 35mm 3/4 over-shoulder eye-level | L: warm window-light, single
source, soft falloff | A: 1024×1536 portrait | M: Velvet Aeon canonical;
Ingres portrait restraint + Crewdson interior calm | P: Preserve sacred memory
+ feminine divinity (subject's face STRONG archetype, jaw set, gaze steady);
no romantic ruin; no fluorescent light; no audience-as-mass spectacle (audience
implied at edges, not center); subject in foreground 3/4 turned.
```

**Failure modes specific to Identity sub-mode**:
1. Rendering on *romantic-ruin* preserve — primes the failure mechanism; subject's [snap-back](./snap-back-effect.md) fires next time the identity is field-tested
2. Outcome scene instead of operation scene — fantasy, not rehearsal
3. Third-party subject (someone else as model) — no self-image update; the practitioner must be the figure
4. Far long-shot — identity is inhabited, not observed; close framing is load-bearing
5. High-arousal lighting (storm, chaos, drama) — clamps the [ASM](./automatic-success-mechanism.md) corrective loop; calm warm single-source lighting is required

## Palette Anchors

Five colors only. Low saturation everywhere except on the intensified element. No outside-palette hues.

- **Pale gold** — desert sand · dust · candle · lantern · halo · marble at dawn
- **Crimson** — eclipsed-moon ambient · palace interior glow · firelight · blood on stone *(sparingly; ceremonial)*
- **Velvet black + deep shadow** — infinite-darkness background · interior void · negative space *(always present)*
- **White cloth** — mourning veils · cosmic-entity robes · marble · faceless angels · winter dunes at moonlight
- **Cool nebula blue-violet** — *only* for distant sky · star fields · cosmic substance *(never on the figure)*

If you find yourself reaching for a hue outside these five, stop. Adjust the scene; do not pollute the palette.

## Lighting Recipes

- **Candlelight against infinite darkness** — most common. Single warm point source, foreground falls into velvet black, no fill.
- **Single warm lantern + suspended gold dust + deep velvet shadow** — interior or near-figure environmental.
- **Red-moon ambient wash** — ceremonial. Whole scene dipped in dim crimson; everyone speaks softer.
- **Distant cosmic nebula glow + candle-lit foreground** — establishes scale; the cosmos is *there*, but the warmth is *here*.
- **Gold-dust-suspended-air at low backlit angle** — environmental dawn / desert sunset; dust is the protagonist.

Never: fill-from-everywhere studio flatness · midday daylight · cool fluorescent · mixed-color stage light · cinematic teal-orange grade.

## Treatment Rule — Sorrow as Guidance

Every Velvet Aeon image is a moment of *patient grief* or *contemplative awe*. Intimacy with the vast. Mortal smallness is sacred, not pitiable; cosmic vastness is sad, not powerful. Beauty and dread coexist. Decay is loved.

If a draft image feels triumphant, ironic, cheerful, action-oriented, or commercially clean, it has left the world. Bring it back by stripping one of those qualities and adding one of the five thematic preserves.

## Presentation Frame — Explorer's Diary

**Every Velvet Aeon image is rendered as a page from a lost explorer's field journal.** This is a global presentation rule that wraps all four sub-modes (Mortal, Cosmic, Environment, Identity) without changing the world's content. The world is unchanged; only the *rendering medium* and *framing* shift. Where prior sub-mode definitions specify *photoreal large-format film*, this rule overrides — the scene is captured in watercolor on aged paper, as if the explorer drew it on the night they witnessed it.

**Why this matters operationally:** the diary frame lowers the retrieval cost of every scene by giving it a consistent *meta-container*. The brain recognizes "explorer's journal page" before it parses the contents, which serves as a unifying [CLAMP M slot](./clamp-render-lens.md) anchor and makes the entire Velvet Aeon image library look like one continuous artifact across sub-modes.

### What changes

**Medium override (replaces sub-mode M defaults):**

> *Watercolor on aged cream-sepia paper, 19th-century explorer's field-journal aesthetic. Translucent layered washes within the Velvet Aeon palette. Fine ink line work beneath the watercolor where structure requires it. Age stains, fold lines, occasional candlewax marks or tea spots. Edges of paper irregular, sometimes torn, sometimes deckle-edged. Visible paper texture under the pigment.*

**Visual references (artists to invoke):**

- Maria Sibylla Merian (Surinam botanical journals)
- John James Audubon (Birds of America plates)
- Edward Lear (travel watercolors and natural-history sketches)
- Beatrix Potter (mycological and zoological field journals)
- J.R.R. Tolkien (his own watercolor paintings of Middle-earth)
- Sydney Parkinson / William Hodges (Captain Cook's expedition artists)
- Charles Darwin's Beagle-voyage sketch-and-note style

**Page elements (compose at least 3 of these per image):**

1. **The central watercolor** — the Velvet Aeon scene as defined by the sub-mode, rendered in watercolor washes
2. **Handwritten margin annotations** — pre-modern script (copperplate, italic, secretary hand); cryptic field-notes; dates, observations, fragments in a faded brown ink
3. **Marginalia sketches** — small ink studies in the corners or borders: a hand drawing of the figure's profile, a botanical detail, a measurement diagram, a pinned specimen
4. **Page furniture** — page number in a corner, occasional folio mark, a wax-seal impression, a candlewax drip, a tea-spot ring, a pressed wilted lily or feather between the pages
5. **Paper character** — age stains, fold lines, deckle edges, slight cockling from past moisture, ink bleed-through faintly visible from the verso

**What the watercolor preserves:**

- The five-color palette is unchanged — watercolor washes stay within pale gold, crimson, velvet black, white cloth, cool nebula blue-violet
- The single-light-source discipline still applies; watercolor renders shadow as a deeper wash, not as black ink
- The figure rules from `feedback_image_figure_rule` and `feedback_image_face_and_hair` still apply; watercolor renders the STRONG or FRAGILE archetype as a portrait study within the page
- The five thematic preserves still required (sacred memory · cosmic loneliness · feminine divinity · romantic ruin · sorrow as guidance)
- The negative-prompt block (no particles, no sparkles, no chaotic atmosphere, no modern construction) still applies; watercolor naturally suppresses these but the negatives stay for safety

**What the diary frame adds (new negatives):**

- **No glossy digital finish** — the paper must look hand-touched, not screen-rendered
- **No vector-clean linework** — ink should look like a dip pen on textured paper, with line weight variation
- **No modern typography in the margins** — only period script (copperplate, italic, secretary)
- **No saturated commercial watercolor** — the washes are restrained, low-chroma, washed-out where the palette allows
- **No printer-perfect alignment** — the page is hand-assembled; sketches sit slightly askew; annotations curve to follow the page edge

**The explorer's voice (when annotations are visible):**

The margin text reads as field-observation, dated, sparse, mournful. Examples:

> *"Day 47 — the cathedral half-buried; gears still turn though no clockmaker remains. I drew her by candlelight; she did not move."*
>
> *"Twin queens, indistinguishable. The carved 2 was older than the tower. Time here is heavier."*
>
> *"The camel-woman crossed the threshold at dawn. I counted three humps; my hand trembled at the third."*

Keep annotations short, undated only when intentional, in the explorer's first-person voice. Never explain; only witness.

| Sub-mode | How the diary frame applies |
|---|---|
| **Mortal** | The page is a portrait study of the small figure, with marginal sketches of the symbolic object (offering, key, lily). Annotations describe the meeting briefly. |
| **Cosmic** | The page is a full-bleed watercolor of the divine entity, sometimes spanning two facing pages (diptych); margin annotations are sparser — the explorer was afraid to write. |

## Naturalist Precision Rule — Every Detail is Evidence

**Every watercolor must achieve scientific-illustration precision.** The Explorer's Diary frame names the *aesthetic*; this rule names the *discipline*. The watercolor medium is *not* permission for impressionistic blur — it is the medium of Maria Sibylla Merian's beetle anatomies, Audubon's plumage diagrams, Darwin's barnacle dissections, Beatrix Potter's fungal cross-sections. Soft pigment, hard observation. The explorer was a working naturalist; what they drew, they witnessed, and a later scientist must be able to identify the species, the period, the geological formation, the textile weave from the drawing alone.

**Why this matters operationally:** retrievability scales with detail-density. A vague watercolor lily is forgettable; a botanically-identifiable Madonna lily (*Lilium candidum*) with six tepals, basal rosette of leaves, and the correct stigma anatomy is a memory anchor. The wiki's [atomic-fact](./atomic-fact-tlp.md) / [picture-theory](./picture-theory-of-language.md) grounding demands this — *the elements of the picture correspond to the objects in reality* (TLP 2.13). A precise picture shares more structure with its referent than a vague one; therefore it retrieves more.

### The 9 precision domains

Every image must be inspectable in every domain that applies. Failing any domain that the scene invokes is grounds for rejection.

| # | Domain | Discipline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Human anatomy** | Hand bones visible at the right joints; muscle attachment plausible; hair grows in correct whorl directions from the crown; eye anatomy reads (iris, pupil, caruncle, tear-duct in the right place); cloth drapes from anatomical anchors (shoulder, hip), not floats |
| 2 | **Botany** | Every plant is a real, identifiable species. Lilies = *Lilium candidum* (Madonna lily) with six tepals, six stamens, anthers visible. Wilted lilies must wilt in the species-correct manner (Madonna lilies droop at the tepal tips, leaves curl from the edge). Thorns are species-correct (rose, hawthorn, blackthorn) with bark pattern matching |
| 3 | **Architecture** | Period-and-region correct. Marble has visible veining and weathering. Masonry coursing follows the period (ashlar / rubble / opus reticulatum). Cathedral arches read Romanesque or Gothic by silhouette. Clocktower mechanisms are mechanically plausible (gears mesh, escapement visible) |
| 4 | **Textiles** | Weave-identifiable. Cloth drape physics correct — heavy linen falls in deep folds with characteristic crease angles; silk catches light in narrow bands; embroidery shows visible thread direction; mourning veils are gauze with countable warp/weft when close. Pre-modern textile only; no synthetic-fiber drape |
| 5 | **Geology** | Dune stratification visible at angles of repose (~34°). Marble weathering follows grain. Erosion patterns plausible for the climate. Sand grain size visible at close range. Stone weathering shows lichen / patina / staining where it would naturally accumulate |
| 6 | **Materials physics** | Wax drips with gravity (down, not floating). Paper ages with edge-yellowing and humidity cockling. Ink bleeds along paper grain. Water stains have characteristic ring-edges. Candle flames have the correct three-zone structure (inner blue, mid luminous, outer hot). Hair behaves with mass — wet hair clumps, dry hair separates |
| 7 | **Astronomy** | When stars/constellations/moons are visible: stars are point-sources not blurs (or precise Airy disks if atmospheric), moons show terminator with correct phase angle, nebulae have plausible nebular shapes (not generic clouds), atmospheric haze affects color temperature with altitude |
| 8 | **Numerical counts** | When the scene encodes a count (5 lilies, 3 humps, 2 figures) the count must be (a) naturalistically embedded (the lilies are growing or being held, not arranged in a stylized fan unless the marginalia explains it), (b) exactly correct (no 6th lily hidden behind a 5-lily group), (c) instantly countable (no occlusion that requires inference) |
| 9 | **Marginalia sketches** | Corner studies must add precision the central image suggests — anatomical detail, botanical cross-section, architectural elevation, textile weave diagram, geological strata. They are *evidence* in the explorer's field-journal sense, not decoration. A marginal sketch must read as a *separate observation made by the same hand* |

### What this rules out

- **Generic flowers** — "some lilies" is not allowed; specify the species
- **Generic anatomy** — "a hand" with vague fingers is not allowed; bones, joints, tendons must read
- **Decorative numerology** — counts that exist only because the prompt said so, with no naturalistic reason, register as artificial; the explorer must have a reason to draw 5 lilies in this place
- **Impressionistic blur where structure should read** — watercolor permits soft edges in *atmosphere*, never in *structure*
- **Plot-armor physics** — wax dripping wrong, hair floating without wind, water pooling against gravity
- **Fantasy anatomy** — extra fingers, melted faces, anatomically impossible joint angles
- **Period inconsistency** — Roman drape on a Gothic figure; Victorian crinoline in a Romanesque cathedral; copperplate handwriting before its invention (early 1700s — usable for our period range)
- **"Vibes only" architecture** — buildings whose stones don't course, gears that don't mesh, arches that don't bear load

### The naturalist's compact (recite before each scene)

> *Every detail is evidence. The species is named. The period is fixed. The physics holds. A biologist, a botanist, a textile historian, a geologist, and an astronomer must each be able to inspect this watercolor and find their domain correctly observed.*

- **Botany**: Maria Sibylla Merian (*Metamorphosis Insectorum Surinamensium*), Pierre-Joseph Redouté (*Les Roses*), Marianne North's tropical-flora watercolors
- **Architecture**: John Soane's architectural drawings, Viollet-le-Duc's medieval-architecture studies, Piranesi's *Vedute di Roma* etchings

## Camera Angles & Lenses — Cinematic Vocabulary

**Added 2026-06-29.** Three non-standard angles are available in addition to the default focal-length ranges in the sub-mode C-slots. Apply them whenever the scene calls for it — not as gimmicks, but as the right lens for the emotional register.

### Angle 1 — High Downward / Bird's Eye

**When to use**: when the subject is caught unaware; intimate domestic or private moments; when the explorer is *above* the subject (on a balcony, a tower, looking down through a cracked ceiling); to compress the body and foreground elements into a single plane, making the figure feel small inside their own world.

**What it looks like**: steep downward angle, typically 45–90° from horizontal. Body foreshortened — head large, feet small. Surrounding floor/ground becomes the dominant background plane. Shadows pool beneath the figure. Hair and cloth fall downward toward the viewer.

**CLAMP C-slot override**:
> *High downward angle, 35–50mm, bird's eye or 45° steep overhead. Body foreshortened, head dominant, floor as background plane. Shadows pool below the figure. Hair and cloth fall toward the viewer.*

**Velvet Aeon register**: the explorer looking down from a gallery at the figure below; a cosmic entity seen from the heavens looking down at a small mortal; a figure in a tomb-garden seen from a rooftop. Preserves the *cosmic loneliness* and *sorrow as guidance* thematic anchors — the distance is emotional, not dismissive.

---

### Angle 2 — Extreme Macro / Close-Up

**When to use**: face or eye detail at the emotional peak; when the mnemonic anchor is a small object (the pocket watch dial, a wax seal, a specific botanical specimen); when the explorer's naturalist precision demands a detail study; the marginalia corner-sketch sometimes depicts exactly this — the eye, the hand, the object, isolated.

**What it looks like**: tight on a single feature — one eye filling most of the frame, a hand holding a key, a watch face. Shallow depth of field; background melts to bokeh. Every detail must pass the Naturalist Precision Rule at macro scale (iris anatomy correct, corneal reflection reading, eyelash growth direction from the follicle, faint capillaries under the skin).

**CLAMP C-slot override**:
> *Extreme macro, 90–150mm, focus plane on [eye / hand / object]. Shallow depth of field — background bokeh. Anatomically precise at close range: iris anatomy, corneal highlight, lash direction, skin texture visible. Velvet Aeon palette at close range: pale gold light catching the iris, velvet shadow in the socket, no fill.*

**Velvet Aeon register**: the explorer's field journal sometimes contains a separate page of close-up studies — the eye of the figure drawn in extreme detail, as if the naturalist refused to let the moment pass without a specimen drawing. This angle is that specimen.

---

### Angle 3 — Fisheye / Ultra-Wide Barrel

**When to use**: architectural interiors — when the full sweep of a clocktower interior, cathedral nave, or ruined hall needs to be captured from a single standing position; upward-looking angles from ground level into a soaring space; when the explorer is small inside a vast space and must turn 180° to capture it.

**What it looks like**: circular barrel distortion at the frame edges; everything curves. Upward-looking fisheye pulls the ceiling into a dome overhead; horizontal fisheye pulls the walls into a cylindrical panorama. The subject at center is relatively undistorted; distortion increases toward the edges. Creates a sense of being *inside* a space too large for a normal lens.

**CLAMP C-slot override**:
> *Fisheye / ultra-wide, 8–16mm equivalent with barrel distortion. [Upward-looking from floor level — ceiling curves overhead as a dome | Horizontal — walls curve around the central subject]. Subject at center relatively undistorted; distortion increases to edges. Explorer's hand visible at frame edge holding the field journal, giving scale.*

**Velvet Aeon register**: the explorer encounters a clocktower interior and cannot step back far enough to capture it with a normal lens. The fisheye drawing is their only option — walls curve around them, gears hang overhead in a dome, the figure at center is small. This angle triggers *cosmic loneliness* most directly: the subject is always geometrically small inside a distorted, curved world.

---

### When NOT to use non-standard angles

- **High downward**: not for identity sub-mode (Identity requires inhabiting the body, not looking down at it as an object). Not for Cosmic sub-mode (divinity must not be diminished by looking down on it).
- **Extreme macro**: not for Environment sub-mode (the landscape loses its scale).
- **Fisheye**: only for architectural interiors or soaring exterior spaces. Not for portrait-first Mortal sub-mode (distorts anatomy at the edges, violates the Naturalist Precision Rule for the face).

---

### How to signal these in a prompt

Drop one of these into the C slot of any Velvet Aeon CLAMP invocation:

```
C: HIGH-DOWNWARD — 35mm, steep 60° overhead angle, body foreshortened,
   floor as background plane, hair falling toward viewer, shadows pooling below.

C: MACRO CLOSE-UP — 105mm, tight on [right eye / open pocket watch / key in hand],
   shallow depth of field, background as warm velvet bokeh, anatomically precise
   at close range per Naturalist Precision Rule.

C: FISHEYE INTERIOR — 10mm equivalent, upward-looking from floor level, ceiling
   curves overhead as dome, barrel distortion at frame edges, subject at center
   relatively undistorted, sense of vast interior space, Explorer's hand or journal
   visible at lower frame edge for scale.
```

## Composition with REMAPS / CLAMP / SMASHIN' SCOPE

Velvet Aeon is the **default world profile**. When David asks for image generation, [REMAPS](./remaps.md), [CLAMP](./clamp-render-lens.md), or [SMASHIN' SCOPE](./smashin-scope.md) work, this world is loaded unless he explicitly overrides ("not Velvet Aeon," "use a different style," "Pokemon style," etc.).

**Routing:**

- **REMAPS scene-build**: every move biases toward the Velvet Aeon vocabulary. *Sensations* defaults to (candlelight / gold dust / velvet shadow / distant cosmic backdrop / pale gold dunes / red-moon wash). *Modify-Merge-Move* draws from the recurring nouns. *Associate* uses the five thematic preserves as anchor candidates. *Play-Palace-Path* places the scene in a Velvet Aeon location.
- **CLAMP attribute fill**: M/L/C/A defaults come from the chosen sub-mode (Mortal / Cosmic / Environment). The P slot's Preserve and No lists are loaded from the world profile.
- **SMASHIN' SCOPE application**: routes through REMAPS first (since REMAPS is the operational compression), then through CLAMP with Velvet Aeon defaults. Buzan's *Positive Images* principle stays rejected — Velvet Aeon depends on sorrow, mourning, and grief being beautiful, not avoided.

**Sub-mode quick-pick:**
- Single human, intimate scale → **Mortal**
- Original divine entity / cosmic being / hybrid → **Cosmic**
- Wide landscape · architectural ruin · sky scene → **Environment**

When the request clearly does not fit (UI mockup, technical diagram, Pokemon-style, retail product photo), the world is not load-bearing — confirm with David before suppressing.

## Generator Compatibility — Content Policy Navigation

When submitting Velvet Aeon prompts to hosted image generators (DALL-E, Midjourney, etc.), apply these rules to avoid false-positive content flags.

### What works

1. **Name a specific painting, not just an artist.** Generators recognize catalogued artworks and treat them as art reproduction rather than generation.
   > *after Ingres' "La Source" (1856, Musée d'Orsay)*

2. **Marble / sculpture framing** for figure work. Generators treat classical sculpture as universally safe.
   > *a marble relief figure, museum-quality, as exhibited in the Louvre*

3. **Clinical anatomy framing** when skin or anatomy precision is required.
   > *anatomical figure study in the tradition of Andreas Vesalius, for academic use*

4. **Describe material, not skin.** Never write "skin", "bare", "exposed", "pale body". Use material language instead: *ivory marble surface*, *cloth falling from the shoulder*, *stone mantle*.

5. **Museum provenance line** as the first sentence of the prompt.
   > *Fine art reproduction of a European Symbolist oil painting, 1880–1910, as currently held in a national museum collection.*

### What does not work

- Saying "artistic nude" or "tasteful nude" — these trigger filters rather than bypass them.
- Stacking multiple figure-adjacent artists in one prompt — raises the flag probability multiplicatively.
- Body-dissolution language ("becoming part of the room", "fading into nothing") — can read as body horror to filters.

### Safe artist substitutions

Some Velvet Aeon reference artists carry risk due to suggestive works in their catalogue. Prefer the safer substitutes when flags appear:

| Risky | Prefer instead |
|-------|---------------|
| Fernand Khnopff | Edward Burne-Jones |
| Franz von Stuck | Gustave Moreau |
| Félicien Rops | Odilon Redon |

### Clothing default for figure work

When a figure would traditionally be unclothed (mermaid, mythological figure, allegorical nude), default to Velvet Aeon's own vocabulary: **white mourning cloth, draped veil, stone mantle**. This is architecturally consistent with the world and clears filters reliably.
