# Wiki Ingest Meta-Pattern (extracted from the 9-wave logic ingest)

**Summary**: A **meta-page** extracting the pattern of the wiki's own ingest pipeline, surfaced from doing **9 sequential waves on the logic domain in a single day** (2026-05-25). The pattern is **operational**: it names what worked, what stalled, what triggered productive vs unproductive expansion, and what the saturation signature looks like. **Useful for future ingests across other domains** — the meta-pattern transfers. **This page is the wiki's most-explicit articulation of how the multi-wave ingest pipeline operates** and at what point it should stop.

## One-line

> The wiki's ingest pipeline runs a **multi-wave depth-first expansion** with explicit promotion rules, cross-domain extension patterns, and a clear saturation signature. **9 waves on logic in a single day produced 60 pages**; the meta-pattern of the ingest itself is now articulable + transferable.

## Unlocks (lead, not footer)

1. **The multi-wave ingest pipeline is a discoverable operational pattern.** Before the 9-wave logic ingest, the wiki's ingest workflow (CLAUDE.md §Ingest workflow) was a *single-wave* 7-step pipeline. **The 9-wave run revealed a more-complex iterative pattern**: each wave has its own theme, depth target, and termination signal. **The wiki's ingest pipeline scales to multi-wave ingests when source-richness + cross-domain connections warrant it.**

2. **Each wave has a distinct character.** The 9 waves on logic exhibited 5 recognizable wave-types:
   - **Wave 1** = Foundation (source summaries + keystone concepts + hub).
   - **Waves 2-4** = Depth-first deepening (more concept pages; architectural-primitive promotion; character bios; chapter completion).
   - **Wave 5** = Bridging (cross-domain links to other wiki layers — Bible-TLP cross-link).
   - **Wave 6** = Synthesis (cross-domain comparison; worked examples; retrospective).
   - **Waves 7-9** = Extension (cross-domain applications; provisional supplements; per-rule continuation).
   
   **The wave-types are reusable for future ingests.** Future multi-wave ingests should follow roughly this sequence.

3. **Architectural-primitive promotion is the deepest unlock mechanism.** Across the 9 waves, **2 architectural primitives were promoted** (recognition-gym → Wave 2; internal-limits → Wave 4; cross-tradition convergence → Wave 7). Each promotion required hitting the N=3 instance threshold + writing the owner page extracting the invariant. **Promotion is the wiki's mechanism for converting recurrent patterns into reusable infrastructure.**

4. **Saturation is signaled explicitly + ignored at peril.** The Wave 8 log entry explicitly noted: *"Future waves should be highly selective."* **Wave 9 partly followed this signal** (4 pages instead of 5; explicit framing of diminishing returns). **Future ingests should track + respect saturation signals.** Continuing past saturation produces low-quality pages that obscure the well-formed core.

5. **The 9-wave run is the wiki's strongest single-domain ingest** by all measures: pages (60), architectural-primitive promotions (2), cross-domain applications (substrate thesis to 4 domains: math + AI + theology + physics; cross-tradition convergence with 4 worked instances), and meta-retrospection (this page). **Future ingests calibrate against this benchmark.**

## Mnemonic

**F-D-B-S-E** = *Foundation · Deepening · Bridging · Synthesis · Extension* — the 5 wave-types.

For the 9-wave total: **9 × 6.7 ≈ 60** — 9 waves averaged ~7 pages each, totaling 60 pages.

**Wave-type definitions**:

- **FOUNDATION (F)**: Source summary pages (one per source PDF). 5-10 keystone concept pages capturing the core ideas + structures. Atomic-design hub page if applicable. Initial glossary registration.
- **DEEPENING (D)**: More concept pages (covering remaining major ideas + cross-references). Architectural-primitive promotion when N=3 instance threshold hit. Character biographies (when source includes biographical content). Chapter-by-chapter completion (when source has well-defined chapters).
- **BRIDGING (B)**: Cross-link to existing wiki layers (Bible, problem-solving, money, memory). Identification of cross-tradition or cross-domain parallels. Provisional supplements flagged for future sources.
- **SYNTHESIS (S)**: Cross-domain comparison pages. Worked-example pages. Meta-retrospective.
- **EXTENSION (E)**: Cross-domain applications of frameworks to other domains. Per-rule / per-instance continuations following established templates. Additional provisional supplements. Additional cross-tradition convergence worked instances.

**Saturation signatures**:

- Cross-references becoming redundant.
- Diminishing novel content per page.
- New pages requiring canonical-knowledge content rather than source-PDF content.
- Cross-domain extension repeating prior wave's themes.
- Per-rule continuations becoming mechanical.
- The wave's pages would be valuable but represent diminishing returns vs additional work in a different domain.

When saturation fires: STOP adding pages; either close the domain (no more waves) or wait for new source PDFs to enable substantive continuation.

The 9-wave structure with its 5 wave-types + the saturation signature is the operational pattern.

## The 5 wave-types in detail

### Foundation (Wave 1 — 9 pages)

**Purpose**: establish the domain's core scaffolding.

**Contents**:
- Source-summary pages (3): one per source PDF.
- Keystone concept pages (6): argument-anatomy · validity-vs-soundness · fallacy-taxonomy · picture-theory · show-vs-say · logic-atomic-design hub.
- Glossary registration of all canonical terms.
- Cross-linking to existing wiki layers where obvious.

**Termination signal**: source PDFs are absorbed; the domain's core structure is articulable + cross-linked.

**Future ingests**: any new domain ingest should start with Foundation wave. Without it, subsequent waves have nothing to build on.

### Deepening (Waves 2-4 — 28 pages combined)

**Purpose**: extend the domain to operational depth + identify architectural-primitive candidates.

**Contents**:
- More concept pages covering remaining major ideas (Wave 2: 9; Wave 3: 11; Wave 4: 8).
- Character biographies (Waves 3-4: 7 figures total).
- Chapter-by-chapter source coverage (Waves 3-4 covered Copi Ch 1-14 + TLP all 7 propositions).
- Architectural-primitive promotion (Wave 2: recognition-gym; Wave 4: internal-limits).
- Russell's TLP introduction + TLP navigation aid (Waves 3-4).

**Termination signal**: source PDFs have been thoroughly covered; pages cover ≥80% of source content; cross-references are dense; architectural primitives have been identified + promoted where applicable.

**Future ingests**: Deepening is where the bulk of the wave-work happens. 2-4 waves of Deepening is typical for a substantial domain (one source PDF = 1-2 Deepening waves; 3 source PDFs = 3-4 Deepening waves).

### Bridging (Wave 5 — 4 pages)

**Purpose**: connect the domain to existing wiki layers + identify cross-domain parallels.

**Contents**:
- Wittgenstein late-period (provisional from canonical knowledge; closes Wave 4 gap).
- Bible-TLP cross-link (Hebrews 11:1 hypostasis-elenchos × TLP show-vs-say).
- TLP navigation aid.

**Termination signal**: major cross-domain parallels identified; cross-links to other wiki layers established.

**Future ingests**: Bridging is critical for any domain that has cross-tradition or cross-layer parallels (logic does, given its substrate-of-other-domains role). For domains without strong cross-layer connections, Bridging may be a smaller wave.

### Synthesis (Wave 6 — 5 pages)

**Purpose**: synthesize across all prior waves + extract meta-patterns + create worked-example pages.

**Contents**:
- Cross-domain synthesis ([logic-among-the-atomic-design-domains](./logic-among-the-atomic-design-domains.md)).
- Worked examples (3 pages: syllogism + natural-deduction + argument-extraction).
- Retrospective ([logic-ingest-retrospective](./logic-ingest-retrospective.md)).

**Termination signal**: the domain is structurally complete; the retrospective captures what was learned.

**Future ingests**: Synthesis is critical for any multi-wave ingest of substantial complexity. Without it, the prior waves' content remains poorly integrated.

### Extension (Waves 7-9 — 14 pages combined)

**Purpose**: apply the domain's frameworks to other domains + continue per-rule / per-instance templates + add provisional supplements.

**Contents (Wave 7)**:
- Cross-domain applications to AI safety (substrate thesis + internal limits).
- META-pattern promotion (cross-tradition convergence to 5th primitive).
- Provisional modal-logic primer.
- Per-rule MP exemplar.

**Contents (Wave 8)**:
- Per-rule MT (completes MP/MT duo).
- 2 cross-tradition convergence worked instances (unmoved-mover + meditative-witness).
- Provisional Bayesian primer.
- Substrate thesis applied to theology.

**Contents (Wave 9)**:
- Cross-tradition convergence to karmic-cycle (4th worked instance).
- Substrate thesis applied to theoretical physics (4th domain).
- Per-rule HS (completes MP/MT/HS trio).
- This meta-pattern page.

**Termination signal**: cross-domain applications become repetitive; per-rule / per-instance continuations become mechanical; novel-content-per-page declines; further work requires new source PDFs.

**Future ingests**: Extension is where saturation typically becomes salient. The Wave 8 log entry flagged saturation; Wave 9 respected it by writing 4 pages instead of 5. **Future Extension waves should track saturation signals carefully.**

## Architectural-primitive promotion as the deepest unlock

The wiki's [composability-index](./composability-index.md) tracks candidate-patterns that may become *architectural primitives* — domain-portable structural patterns that recur across multiple specific applications.

**Promotion rule**: when a candidate-pattern accumulates **N=3 instances** across substantially different domains or applications, it becomes eligible for promotion to an *owner page* extracting the invariant.

**The 9-wave logic ingest produced 3 architectural-primitive promotions**:

1. **[recognition-gym-pattern](./recognition-gym-pattern.md)** (Wave 2): from candidate (N=2 with construct + crux recognition gyms) to confirmed primitive (N=3 with the new Fallacy-Recognition Gym from [fallacy-taxonomy](./fallacy-taxonomy.md)).

2. **[internal-limits-pattern](./internal-limits-pattern.md)** (Wave 4): from candidate (the implicit pattern across [godels-incompleteness](./godels-incompleteness.md) + [truth-function-machine](./truth-function-machine.md) + [limits-of-language-tlp](./limits-of-language-tlp.md)) to confirmed primitive (N=5 with language + math + computation + physics + phenomenology instances).

3. **[cross-tradition-convergence-pattern](./cross-tradition-convergence-pattern.md)** (Wave 7): from Wave 5's candidate-pattern (identified in [hypostasis-elenchos-and-tlp-show-vs-say](./hypostasis-elenchos-and-tlp-show-vs-say.md)) to confirmed primitive with 5+ tradition instances on show-vs-say. Later extended to 4 worked instances total across Waves 7-9 (show-vs-say + unmoved-mover + meditative-witness + karmic-cycle).

**Promotion is the wiki's mechanism for converting recurrent patterns into reusable infrastructure.** Once a primitive has an owner page, future ingests can recognize new instances quickly + add them to the primitive's instance count rather than re-inventing the structure.

**Future ingests**: should explicitly track candidate-patterns + plan for promotion when N=3 thresholds are hit. Promotion typically requires:
- An owner page extracting the invariant.
- Updates to [composability-index](./composability-index.md) §Patterns currently registered.
- Cross-references from each instance back to the primitive.

## Saturation: signature + response

**Saturation signature** (observed in the 9-wave logic ingest):

1. **Cross-references becoming redundant**: by Wave 6, many pages were cross-linking to many other pages; novel cross-links per new page declined.

2. **Diminishing novel content per page**: Wave 1 pages averaged ~3000 words of mostly-novel content; Wave 8-9 pages averaged ~3000 words but with progressively more reference to prior content.

3. **New pages requiring canonical-knowledge content**: Wave 7-8 added several pages built from canonical knowledge rather than source-PDF content ([modal-logic-primer](./modal-logic-primer.md), [bayesian-probability-primer](./bayesian-probability-primer.md)). **Pure source-derived content was substantially exhausted by Wave 6.**

4. **Cross-domain extension repeating prior themes**: by Wave 9, the substrate-thesis × theoretical-physics page (Wave 9) was structurally similar to the substrate-thesis × theology page (Wave 8) which was structurally similar to the substrate-thesis × AI alignment page (Wave 7). **The pattern was being applied without producing substantially new operational insights.**

5. **Per-rule continuations becoming mechanical**: by HS (Wave 9), the per-rule template's value per page had declined from MP (Wave 7) onward.

**Saturation response** (operational rule):

- **When 2+ saturation signals fire**: explicitly flag in the log entry.
- **When 3+ saturation signals fire**: write the wave with fewer pages + explicit acknowledgment of diminishing returns.
- **When 4+ saturation signals fire**: stop the multi-wave run; consume the existing pages; await new source PDFs or new wiki-level priorities before resuming.

**Wave 8** flagged 2-3 saturation signals; **Wave 9** has 4+ signals firing. This page acknowledges the signal explicitly. **Wave 10+ should not proceed without new source PDFs or substantive new wiki-level priorities.**

## What this 9-wave run did NOT do

Important boundaries:

- **Did not exhaust the logic domain.** Future source PDFs (Smullyan model theory, Sider modal logic, Pierce or Sørensen-Urzyczyn type theory, Jaynes Bayesian, Goldstein Gödel narrative, Wittgenstein *Investigations*) would substantially extend the domain.
- **Did not solve any open problems.** The wiki added structured pages on existing scholarship; it did not produce novel proofs, novel philosophical insights, or novel empirical claims.
- **Did not cover all of philosophy of logic.** Domains like philosophical logic (Kripke on naming, Lewis on counterfactuals), informal logic theory, argumentation theory, computational logic engineering practice, automated theorem proving, etc., are not deeply covered.
- **Did not produce a textbook.** The 60 pages are interconnected encyclopedic entries, not a coherent learning sequence. (Though Wave 5's [tractatus-proposition-tree](./tractatus-proposition-tree.md) + reading-paths in [logic-atomic-design](./logic-atomic-design.md) provide partial sequences.)
- **Did not produce an authoritative reference on any single topic.** Each page is a wiki-quality entry, not a peer-reviewed authoritative source. Source-discipline flags are explicit throughout.

## Operational implications for future ingests

Future multi-wave ingests should:

1. **Plan around the F-D-B-S-E wave-type sequence** (Foundation, Deepening, Bridging, Synthesis, Extension).

2. **Hit Foundation in Wave 1** — source summaries + keystone concepts + hub.

3. **Aim for 2-4 Deepening waves** depending on source-richness.

4. **Include Bridging** when cross-tradition or cross-layer connections exist.

5. **Always include Synthesis** for any ingest of 3+ waves.

6. **Track architectural-primitive candidates** throughout — be ready to promote when N=3 hits.

7. **Track saturation signals** — flag at 2+, scale back at 3+, stop at 4+.

8. **Don't exceed ~10 waves on a single domain** in a single day. Past that, returns are very low; better to start a new domain ingest.

9. **Include this meta-pattern page (or a domain-specific version) in any 5+ wave ingest** for retrospective + meta-learning.

10. **Respect the user's standing feedback** throughout: mnemonics + checksums + visuals on every page; surface unlocks as leads; cross-tradition insights as load-bearing; depth-first per domain.

## Status — wave 10 should NOT proceed without new sources

**The wiki's recommendation, based on the saturation signature firing 4+ signals in Wave 9**:

- **Wave 10 (and beyond) should not proceed on the logic domain** without new source PDFs (Smullyan, Sider, Pierce / Sørensen-Urzyczyn, Jaynes, Goldstein, *Philosophical Investigations*).
- **Cross-domain extensions** (substrate thesis to additional domains, cross-tradition convergence to additional instances) **are possible but produce diminishing returns**. Each additional application is roughly half as valuable as the prior one because the pattern's META-substantive support is already established.
- **Per-rule continuations** beyond MP/MT/HS produce mechanical pages with low novel content.
- **The most valuable next move** is either:
  - Wait for new source PDFs to enable Wave 10+ with genuinely new content.
  - Start a new domain ingest in a different area (e.g., genuine new source on cybersecurity, medicine, history, computer science, music theory).
  - Pause logic-domain expansion + use existing 60 pages as is.

**This page is the explicit "no more waves without new sources" page.** It's an operational guidance for the wiki's future development.
