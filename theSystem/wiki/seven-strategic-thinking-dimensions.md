# Seven Strategic Thinking Dimensions

**Summary**: Sloan's named **seven dimensions** that every strategic-thinking session should sweep through rounds of [critical inquiry](./nine-dive-question-drill.md) and [critical dialogue](./critical-dialogue-discussion-debate.md). **Outer ring** (the 4 conditioning factors): Finance · Risk Assessment · Technology · Integrity. **Inner ring** (the 3 conditioned outcomes): Business Value · Individual Development · Social Contribution. The 4 outer dimensions shape what, how, and why we think about the 3 inner dimensions. Strategy meetings that only sweep Finance miss six of the seven.

**Sources**:
- [sloan-learning-to-think-strategically](./sloan-learning-to-think-strategically.md) — source summary
- Sloan, *Learning to Think Strategically* (4th ed., 2020), Ch 11 §"Seven dimensions that benefit from critical inquiry", Figure 11.1

**Last updated**: 2026-05-27

## The 7 dimensions

### Outer ring — the 4 conditioning factors

| Dimension | Includes | Sample dive questions |
|---|---|---|
| **Finance** | Economics · budget · profit/loss · investment | What's the financial model assume about growth? Whose forecast? |
| **Risk assessment** | Financial security · physical security · intellectual property | What risks aren't priced in? Whose risk is it really? |
| **Technology** | Hardware · software · integration | What's the technical lock-in? Who controls the platform? |
| **Integrity** | Authenticity · truth · honesty | Are we being honest about the data? About the customer? About ourselves? |

### Inner ring — the 3 conditioned outcomes

| Dimension | Includes | Sample dive questions |
|---|---|---|
| **Business value** | Stakeholder · customers · products · services · expansion · improvement | Who actually benefits? In what timeframe? |
| **Individual development** | Learning as investment · satisfaction · knowledge / thinking / creative capability | What does this strategy do to the people executing it? |
| **Social contribution** | Environment · human rights · arts · health · politics · community development | What does the world become if this strategy succeeds? Fails? |

## Why the ring structure

The outer ring **conditions** the inner ring. Specifically:

- **Finance** decisions shape what Business Value is even pursued
- **Risk assessment** shapes what Individual Development the team is asked to undertake (volatile risk → conservative dev plans; stable risk → ambitious dev plans)
- **Technology** shapes the Social Contribution the strategy can make (technology choices have externalities)
- **Integrity** is the gate above all three inner dimensions; without integrity, Business Value is fraud, Individual Development is exploitation, and Social Contribution is propaganda

Conversely, the inner ring **draws on** the outer ring — every Business Value question implicitly references Finance + Risk + Technology + Integrity.

Sloan's intent in the ring diagram: make the dependency explicit. A strategy session that asks only Finance questions is collapsing 6 of 7 dimensions onto one.

## How to use the 7 dimensions

### Sweep form

Take any strategic problem. For each of the 7 dimensions, ask:
- **How will this decision promote or prevent the strategic intent**, per this dimension?
- **What impact will this decision have on this dimension over a 1-year, 5-year, 25-year horizon?**
- **Where do our competitors stand on this dimension?**
- **Who might see this dimension very differently?** Why?
- **Who gains and who loses from this decision, on this dimension?** By how much? For how long?
- **Who cares about this dimension now? Who will care later?** Why?

For a single problem, the sweep takes 30-90 minutes (the time-cost is why most teams skip it).

### Structured input to the [nine-dive-question-drill](./nine-dive-question-drill.md)

When running the 9-question drill, use the 7 dimensions to **structure question 4 (Other frames?)**. Instead of "what other frames could we use?", ask: "what does the Finance frame say? The Risk frame? The Technology frame? …" through all 7. This converts vague "other frames" into concrete dimension-sweeps.

### Audit form

After a strategy session, score each dimension 0-3:
- 0 = not mentioned
- 1 = mentioned but not interrogated
- 2 = interrogated but no dive-level question landed
- 3 = dive-level question landed and surfaced new content

Pass: ≥4 dimensions at score ≥2; ≥1 dimension at score 3.

Most strategy sessions score 1-2 dimensions at 2-3 (typically Finance + Risk) and 5-6 at 0-1. The audit makes the imbalance visible.

## Integrity as the load-bearing dimension

Sloan emphasizes integrity unusually heavily. Her interview observation:

> When these seven dimensions are included as part of the strategic thinking process, issues of integrity, core competencies, values, and hidden agendas tend to surface early and candidly. The critical inquiry and critical dialogue process tends to illuminate potential incongruence, inconsistency, or misalignment.

The wiki adopts: **Integrity is the surfacer dimension** — it's the dimension most likely to expose the other six. Asking integrity questions ("are we being honest about X?") tends to reveal that Finance assumptions are aspirational, that Risk is being externalized to others, that Technology choices serve hidden agendas. Integrity questions act as a meta-flashlight on the other 6.

## Cross-link to the [5 attributes](./five-attributes-strategic-thinking.md) Juggle attribute

The juggle attribute (handle many things at once) has a structured target: **handle all 7 dimensions simultaneously** rather than collapsing to 1-2. The 7 dimensions give Juggle a concrete ceiling — when a strategist can hold all 7 in active attention, the juggle attribute is at full strength.

## METER

- `dimensions_swept_count` per session (0-7); pass ≥4 dimensions interrogated
- `dimensions_score_3_count` per session; pass ≥1
- `integrity_question_count` per session; pass ≥1 (integrity is structurally undervalued; force it)
- `outer_inner_balance` — ratio of outer-ring questions to inner-ring; pass: roughly 1:1 (chronic over-weighting of outer = strategy without humanity; over-weighting of inner = strategy without rigor)

## Failure modes

1. **Finance-only sweep** — most common; team treats Finance as the strategy and the other 6 as constraints. Defense: explicit dimension scorecard at meeting end.
2. **Integrity-skipping** — integrity is the dimension most likely to be skipped because it surfaces uncomfortable truths. Defense: integrity question is mandatory before adjournment.
3. **Outer-ring-only** — focuses on the 4 conditioning factors but never asks the 3 outcome questions; produces strategically efficient activity with no human meaning.
4. **Inner-ring-only** — values-talk that never grounds in Finance + Risk + Technology + Integrity; produces inspiring strategy that bankrupts the company.
5. **Sweep-fatigue** — doing the 7-dimension sweep on every micro-decision; the discipline collapses from overuse. Defense: reserve for strategic-thinking sessions (Thinking dot of [sloan-triad-model](./sloan-triad-model.md)); skip for operational decisions.

## Worked example — childhood obesity (per Sloan Ch 8 framing)

Sloan opens the framing chapter with the example of childhood obesity. Different framers produce different strategy:

| Framer | Frame | Misses |
|---|---|---|
| Business development | Market for new food + clothing lines | Integrity, Individual Development, Social Contribution |
| Educator | Learning + behavioral disorders | Finance, Risk, Technology |
| Psychologist | Long-term developmental | Finance, Technology |
| Scientist | Population growth outstripping agriculture | Individual Development, Integrity |
| Sociologist | Inadequate family + social structures | Finance, Technology, Risk |
| Economist | Purchasing-power inequality | Individual Development, Technology |

**None of these framers alone** sweeps all 7 dimensions. The strategic-thinking move: convene a session that includes all 7 frames, then sweep all 7 dimensions across all 7 frames. The output is a strategy that holds the polarities ([AND](./and-polarity-thinking.md)) instead of choosing among the framers.

## Mnemonic

A **Velvet Aeon Cosmic-mode dome** with **two concentric rings** of pillars — **4 tall pillars** on the outer ring (Finance · Risk · Technology · Integrity), **3 shorter pillars** on the inner ring (Business Value · Individual Development · Social Contribution). At the dome's center, a **luminous woman** holds a single small bowl. Each pillar projects a **beam of light toward the bowl**; the 4 outer beams pass *through* the 3 inner beams before reaching the bowl, the outer ring **conditioning** the inner. The bowl's contents change color depending on which beams are dominant.

The woman's face is **fragile/luminous** per [feedback-image-face-and-hair](./feedback-image-face-and-hair.md) (strategy as care, not power). The **Integrity pillar** is the **tallest of the 4 outer**; its beam **illuminates the dust** drifting across the other 6 pillars' beams, revealing whether they are clean or contaminated. Long hair lifted in the dome's wind. One overhead moon as single light source. The 7 pillars are arranged so the outer 4 form a square (NSEW) and the inner 3 form a triangle inside — geometrically distinct, visually paired.
